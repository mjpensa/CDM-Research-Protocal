## Pre-Mortem Analysis

### Failure Mode 1: Vendor Obscures Internal Work
**Hypothesis:** Delta Capita announcement dominates coverage; internal capability building goes unreported.

- Probability this applies: 35%
- Mitigation: Search specifically for internal CDM capability separate from vendor
- Look for: Internal teams, proprietary development, parallel initiatives

### Failure Mode 2: 2018 Pilot Misinterpreted
**Hypothesis:** 2018 pilot participation may have been exploratory only, not indicating ongoing capability.

- Probability this applies: 30%
- Mitigation: Search for evidence of continuation from 2018 to present
- Red flag: 2018 evidence only, nothing recent

### Failure Mode 3: Regional Variation
**Hypothesis:** HSBC's approach may vary by region (UK vs. Asia vs. Americas).

- Probability this applies: 25%
- Mitigation: Search for regional-specific CDM approaches
- Implication: Classification may need regional nuance

### Failure Mode 4: False Dichotomy
**Hypothesis:** I may force a binary internal/vendor classification when reality is more nuanced.

- Probability this applies: 40%
- Mitigation: Allow for hybrid classification; document nuance
- Commitment: Capture full picture, not just fit into boxes

### Anticipated Difficulty: MODERATE
- Vendor relationship is known, question is internal capability
- 2018 participation provides historical hook
- Multi-regional complexity
