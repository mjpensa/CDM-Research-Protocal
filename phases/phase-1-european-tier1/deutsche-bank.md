## Pre-Mortem Analysis

### Failure Mode 1: Insufficient Public Information
**Hypothesis:** German banks may be less transparent about technology initiatives than US/UK peers. Deutsche Bank's communications may focus on financial performance rather than operational technology.

- Probability this applies: 40%
- Mitigation: Search investor presentations, annual report technology sections, German-language sources
- Fallback: If no public evidence, cannot confirm pilot claim; classify based on inference

### Failure Mode 2: Misleading Evidence
**Hypothesis:** General statements about "derivatives technology transformation" may be misinterpreted as CDM engagement. Vendor marketing may overstate DB's involvement.

- Probability this applies: 30%
- Mitigation: Require CDM/DRR specific mentions; verify vendor claims with DB sources
- Red flags: Vague "modernization" language, vendor-only sources, marketing tone

### Failure Mode 3: Outdated Information
**Hypothesis:** The "pilot" claim may be based on old information that no longer reflects current status. DB's priorities may have shifted.

- Probability this applies: 25%
- Mitigation: Weight evidence by recency; prioritize 2024-2025 sources
- Time threshold: Evidence must be <18 months old for current classification

### Failure Mode 4: Confirmation Bias
**Hypothesis:** Original framework claims pilot status; I may unconsciously seek confirming evidence.

- Probability this applies: 35%
- Mitigation: Null hypothesis approach; adversarial searches for non-engagement
- Commitment: Will execute specific searches for PRAGMATIST evidence

### Anticipated Difficulty: MODERATE
- Some coverage expected given DB's prominence
- German transparency may limit visibility
- Pilot claim needs specific validation
