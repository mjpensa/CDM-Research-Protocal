## Research Objective

**Primary Goal:** Determine if SocGen is following BNP Paribas's CDM leadership within French banking

**Key Questions:**
1. Has SocGen announced any CDM/DRR initiatives?

## Pre-Mortem Analysis

### Failure Mode 1: BNP Shadow Effect
**Hypothesis:** Media coverage of French bank CDM activity may focus on BNP as the leader, leaving SocGen's activities undocumented even if they exist.

- Probability this applies: 35%
- Mitigation: Search SocGen specifically; check for "following BNP" narratives
- Fallback: Inference from French banking cohort behavior

### Failure Mode 2: Equity vs. Rates Focus
**Hypothesis:** SocGen's strength is equity derivatives; CDM/DRR coverage focuses on rates/credit. SocGen may be engaged but in different product areas.

- Probability this applies: 25%
- Mitigation: Search for equity derivatives CDM applications
- Red flag: Only rates-focused CDM coverage

### Failure Mode 3: Quiet Follower
**Hypothesis:** SocGen may be following BNP quietly without public announcements, making engagement invisible.

- Probability this applies: 40%
- Mitigation: Look for indirect signals (hiring, vendor partnerships, conference attendance)
- Implication: May need to classify based on inference if direct evidence absent

### Failure Mode 4: False BNP Analogy
**Hypothesis:** I may assume SocGen follows BNP when they're actually pursuing different strategies.

- Probability this applies: 30%
- Mitigation: Actively search for differentiation evidence
- Commitment: Test PRAGMATIST hypothesis explicitly

### Anticipated Difficulty: MODERATE-DIFFICULT
- BNP shadow may obscure SocGen-specific coverage
- French banking cohort dynamic creates inference opportunity but also assumption risk
