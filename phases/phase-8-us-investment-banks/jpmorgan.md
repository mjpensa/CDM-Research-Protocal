## Research Objective

**Primary Goal:** Document confirmed ARCHITECT-Native status and understand production scope

**Framework Claim:** ARCHITECT-Native (confirmed production October 2024)

**Critical Context:** JPMorgan announced CDM production in October 2024. This is NOT a discovery research — the classification is established. The research objective is to understand:
1. Scope of production deployment
2. Products/workflows in CDM
3. Governance/leadership role in CDM ecosystem
4. Strategic rationale

**Key Questions:**
1. What is the scope of JPMorgan's CDM production deployment?
2. What products/workflows are in CDM production?
3. Is JPMorgan contributing to CDM governance/development?
4. How does CFTC Rewrite drive their CDM strategy?
5. What is the timeline and roadmap for expanded CDM usage?
