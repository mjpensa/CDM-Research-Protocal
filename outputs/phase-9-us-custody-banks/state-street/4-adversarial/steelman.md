# Steelman Analysis: State Street

**Bank**: State Street Corporation
**Phase**: 9 (US Custody Banks)
**Date**: 2025-12-21

---

## Steelman

The strongest argument for upgrading State Street's classification would be:

"State Street, as a major custodian for derivatives funds and institutional investors, might need CDM capabilities to service clients who trade derivatives. Client demand could drive CDM adoption even if State Street doesn't trade derivatives directly."

---

## Evaluation

**Verdict**: Weak

While this argument has theoretical merit, no evidence supports it:
- No CDM job postings
- No vendor partnerships announced
- No ISDA/FINOS participation
- No client-driven CDM services announced

State Street's technology investments focus on custody platforms (State Street Alpha), data services, and ESG analytics - not derivatives infrastructure.
