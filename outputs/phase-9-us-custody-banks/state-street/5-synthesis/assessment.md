## Business Model Analysis

State Street is one of the world's largest custodian banks with the following focus areas:

1. **Custody Services**: Asset safekeeping and servicing
2. **Investment Management**: State Street Global Advisors (SSGA)
3. **Data Services**: Investment analytics and reporting
4. **Alpha Platform**: Integrated front-to-back investment servicing

None of these core businesses require CDM adoption. State Street's derivatives exposure is minimal and primarily related to:
- Client hedging services (limited)
- Internal treasury operations (standard)
- SSGA fund hedging (managed externally)
