# Steelman Analysis: BNY Mellon

**Bank**: Bank of New York Mellon
**Phase**: 9 (US Custody Banks)
**Date**: 2025-12-21

---

## Steelman

The strongest argument for upgrading BNY Mellon to PRAGMATIST:

"BNY Mellon's FINOS Platinum membership and active open source culture, combined with CFTC enforcement pressure, could motivate CDM adoption. The bank's $3B tech budget and 11,000 technologists provide capacity for CDM implementation. Recurring CFTC fines create strong incentive to adopt standardized reporting."

---

## Evaluation

**Verdict**: Moderate strength, but insufficient evidence

**Analysis**:
1. **FINOS membership is general, not CDM-specific**: Open source engagement focuses on Java ecosystem, not CDM
2. **No vendor relationships detected**: No REGnosys, Axoni, or other CDM vendor partnerships announced
3. **No hiring signals**: No CDM-related job postings despite large technology workforce
4. **CFTC pressure hasn't driven CDM yet**: 2024 fine shows reporting issues persist 5 years after 2019 fine
5. **Custody business model**: Derivatives are not primary business, reducing CDM urgency

**Conclusion**: The steelman argument identifies potential motivators but evidence suggests BNY Mellon has not acted on these pressures with CDM adoption.
