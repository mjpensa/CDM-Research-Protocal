**Bank**: Bank of New York Mellon
**Phase**: 9 (US Custody Banks)
**Date**: 2025-12-21

---

## Proposed Classification

**OBSERVER** (FINOS Platinum Member) with 55% confidence, Maturity 1

---

## Counter-Case: BNY Mellon Could Be Higher

### Argument 1: FINOS Platinum Means Ecosystem Contribution
Platinum membership might indicate PRAGMATIST-level engagement.

**Rebuttal**: FINOS membership categories reflect financial contribution, not technical engagement. BNY Mellon's open source work focuses on Java (Eclipse Collections) rather than CDM.

### Argument 2: Swap Dealer Registration
As a registered swap dealer, BNY Mellon might have internal CDM work.

**Rebuttal**: CFTC enforcement actions in 2019 and 2024 suggest traditional reporting approach, not CDM-enabled automation.

### Argument 3: Technology Leadership
BNY Mellon has $3B tech budget and strong innovation culture.

**Rebuttal**: Technology investment focuses on custody infrastructure (Alpha platform), not derivatives reporting.

---

## Counter-Case: BNY Mellon Could Be Lower

### Argument: Should Be UNKNOWN Like State Street
Custody banks have minimal derivatives exposure.

**Rebuttal**: Unlike State Street, BNY Mellon is a FINOS Platinum Member with verified executive participation. OBSERVER is appropriate for membership without CDM work.

---

## Verdict

**Classification Sustained**: OBSERVER appropriately reflects FINOS membership without CDM specificity.
