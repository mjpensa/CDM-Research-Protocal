## Steelman: Hong Kong Subsidiary CDM Path

### Thesis
Bank of China (Hong Kong) - BOCHK - could adopt CDM when ISDA DRR covers Hong Kong in 2025.

### Supporting Arguments
1. BOCHK is one of Hong Kong's three note-issuing banks
2. ISDA DRR extending to HKMA rules in 2025
3. Hong Kong uses ISDA framework (not NAFMII)
4. BOCHK has international client base

### Counter-Arguments
1. Future trigger, not current evidence
2. No preparation evidence found
3. Per protocol, classify current state

### Assessment
**MODERATE** (25%) - Plausible future path for BOCHK specifically.

**Recommendation**: Re-assess when ISDA DRR Hong Kong goes live.

---

## Steelman: Derivatives Pioneer Advantage

### Thesis
BOC's pioneer status in Chinese derivatives could translate to CDM leadership if China adopts CDM-like standards.

### Supporting Arguments
1. BOC pioneered derivatives in China
2. ISDA-NAFMII collaboration exists
3. China aligning with global standards (LEI, etc.)

### Counter-Arguments
1. Speculative - no current evidence
2. NAFMII has own development path
3. Chinese regulators prefer domestic standards

### Assessment
**WEAK** (10%) - Too speculative for classification impact.

---

## Verdict

Both steelman arguments are about future potential, not current state. UNKNOWN classification at 40% confidence remains appropriate.
