## Framework Analysis

### Why UNKNOWN Classification?

ICBC is classified as UNKNOWN because it operates in a **fundamentally different regulatory framework**:

#### NAFMII Framework (China)
- Mandated by PBoC for all interbank market participants
- Based on ISDA 2002 Master Agreement but adapted for Chinese law
- Covers interest rate, FX, bond, credit, and gold derivatives
- Disputes submitted to Chinese courts or arbitration

#### ISDA CDM Framework (Global)
- Covers CFTC, EMIR, MAS, ASIC, HKMA jurisdictions
- Uses Common Domain Model for machine-executable reporting
- English law or NY law governance typically
- International arbitration or court jurisdiction

### These Frameworks Are Separate
Chinese domestic derivatives transactions do not use ISDA CDM. The frameworks collaborate (e.g., IBOR fallbacks documents) but remain distinct ecosystems.
