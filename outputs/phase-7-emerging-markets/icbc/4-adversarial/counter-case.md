## Counter-Case: ICBC Could Be PRAGMATIST

### Argument
ICBC has US operations (NY branch, ICBC USA NA, broker-dealer) subject to CFTC rules. These entities could be using CDM/DRR for US regulatory reporting.

### Evidence Supporting Counter-Case
1. ICBC has CFTC-regulated US entities
2. CFTC Rewrite affects US swap dealers
3. ISDA DRR covers CFTC reporting jurisdiction

### Weakness of Counter-Case
1. Resolution plan documents mention only hedging derivatives, no CDM
2. US operations described as minimal derivatives activity
3. No evidence of CDM adoption in US regulatory filings
4. Parent bank's NAFMII framework dominates operations

**Assessment**: WEAK (10%) - US operations too small to drive CDM investment

---

## Counter-Case: ICBC Could Be OBSERVER

### Argument
ICBC may have ISDA membership (not verified) which would qualify for OBSERVER.

### Evidence Supporting Counter-Case
1. Major global bank would typically be ISDA member
2. Uses ISDA Master Agreement for cross-border transactions

### Weakness of Counter-Case
1. ISDA membership not confirmed
2. No ISDA Board representation
3. No working group participation found
4. Domestic focus on NAFMII framework

**Assessment**: WEAK (15%) - No verified ISDA engagement

---

## Conclusion

Counter-cases do not overcome the structural explanation for UNKNOWN classification. NAFMII framework separation is the dominant factor.
