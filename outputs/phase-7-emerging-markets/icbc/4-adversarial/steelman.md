# Steelman Analysis: ICBC

**Bank**: Industrial and Commercial Bank of China
**Phase**: 7 (Emerging Markets)
**Date**: 2025-12-21

---

## Steelman: US Operations Using CDM

### Thesis
ICBC's US entities (NY branch, national bank, broker-dealer) are subject to CFTC rules and could be using ISDA DRR for regulatory reporting.

### Supporting Arguments
1. CFTC Rewrite affects swap dealers operating in US
2. ISDA DRR provides cost-effective CFTC compliance
3. ICBC has sophisticated technology capabilities ($3B+ IT investment)
4. JPMorgan (CFTC-regulated) is in CDM production

### Counter-Arguments
1. Resolution plans show minimal derivatives activity in US entities
2. "Derivatives only used for hedging, no material hedges"
3. No CDM evidence in any US regulatory filings
4. Parent bank's NAFMII focus dominates strategy

### Assessment
**WEAK** (10%) - US operations too minimal to warrant CDM investment.

---

## Steelman: Future CDM Adoption

### Thesis
ISDA is expanding DRR to Hong Kong (2025). ICBC's Hong Kong operations could adopt CDM.

### Supporting Arguments
1. ISDA DRR will cover Hong Kong in 2025
2. ICBC has significant Hong Kong presence (ICBC Asia)
3. Hong Kong uses ISDA framework, not NAFMII
4. Regulatory pressure from HKMA

### Counter-Arguments
1. Hong Kong operations smaller than mainland
2. No current evidence of preparation
3. Future adoption ≠ current classification
4. Per protocol, classify based on current evidence

### Assessment
**MODERATE** (25%) - Plausible future path, but does not affect current classification.

---

## Verdict

Both steelman arguments are plausible but do not change current classification. UNKNOWN at 40% confidence remains appropriate.

**Monitoring Recommendation**: Re-assess when ISDA DRR Hong Kong goes live (2025).
