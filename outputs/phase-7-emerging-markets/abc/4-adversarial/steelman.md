## Steelman Case: ABC Is an OBSERVER

### Core Argument

ABC's explicit ISDA primary membership claim, unique among Chinese Big Four banks, indicates genuine engagement with the ISDA ecosystem. While no CDM-specific evidence exists, ABC's ISDA relationship positions it as an OBSERVER of CDM developments, particularly given:

1. **Regulatory Foresight**: ISDA DRR will cover Hong Kong in 2025; ABC's Hong Kong operations are significant
2. **Primary Membership Status**: "Primary member" suggests deeper engagement than basic membership
3. **International Strategy**: ABC's international expansion strategy requires ISDA documentation competency
4. **First Mover Advantage**: Among Chinese banks, ABC may be positioning for early CDM awareness

### Supporting Logic

```
ISDA Primary Membership + International Presence + Regulatory Horizon
= Likely awareness of CDM developments
= OBSERVER classification warranted
```

### Evidence Marshaling

| Evidence | Steelman Interpretation |
|----------|------------------------|
| ABC-E001 (ISDA membership) | Proves active ISDA engagement |
| Hong Kong operations | HKMA jurisdiction = ISDA DRR scope |
| No CDM evidence | "OBSERVER" doesn't require adoption evidence |
| NAFMII framework | Only applies to domestic; international uses ISDA |

## Steelman Strength Assessment

| Factor | Strength |
|--------|----------|
| Evidence quality | Medium - ISDA membership is Tier 2 |
| Logical coherence | Medium - argument is internally consistent |
| Falsifiability | Low - hard to disprove "awareness" |
| Parsimony | Low - requires assumptions beyond evidence |

**Overall Steelman Strength**: **MEDIUM**

The argument is coherent but relies on inference from ISDA membership rather than CDM-specific evidence.

---

## Why Steelman Still Fails

### 1. OBSERVER Requires Active Engagement

Per classification criteria, OBSERVER requires:
- `membership_or_participation` OR `hiring_signal`

ABC has membership (ISDA) but no evidence of CDM-specific participation or hiring.

### 2. ISDA ≠ CDM

ISDA membership predates CDM by 30+ years. Most ISDA members are not CDM adopters or even observers. Membership alone is not CDM evidence.

### 3. Cohort Consistency

Upgrading ABC to OBSERVER while keeping ICBC/BOC/CCB as UNKNOWN would create inconsistency. If ISDA membership warrants OBSERVER for ABC, it should for all four - but no evidence supports this for any.

### 4. Confidence Inflation

Classifying ABC as OBSERVER based on ISDA membership alone would inflate confidence beyond evidence. The steelman case is plausible but not proven.

---

## Verdict

The steelman case is **intellectually interesting but evidentially insufficient**.

The strongest counter-argument (ISDA membership → OBSERVER) fails because:
1. ISDA membership is common and non-specific to CDM
2. No CDM-specific evidence exists to support OBSERVER
3. Classification consistency requires cohort-level justification

**Recommendation**: Maintain UNKNOWN classification
