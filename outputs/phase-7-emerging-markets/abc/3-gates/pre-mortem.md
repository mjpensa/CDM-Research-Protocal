# Pre-Mortem Analysis: ABC

**Bank**: Agricultural Bank of China Limited
**Phase**: 7 (Emerging Markets)
**Date**: 2025-12-21

---

## Pre-Mortem Question

"If this research fails to find accurate CDM adoption evidence for ABC, what will have caused the failure?"

---

## Potential Failure Modes

### 1. Cohort Assumption Error (HIGH RISK - MITIGATED)

**Failure Mode**: Assuming ABC follows ICBC/BOC/CCB pattern without verifying

**Mitigation Applied**:
- Conducted independent Tier 1 and Tier 2 searches for ABC specifically
- Discovered unique evidence: ABC explicitly claims ISDA primary membership
- Verified same structural constraints (NAFMII framework) apply

**Residual Risk**: Low - unique evidence found but same conclusion reached

---

### 2. International Subsidiary Blind Spot (MEDIUM RISK)

**Failure Mode**: Missing CDM adoption in ABC's international operations (Hong Kong, London, New York branches)

**Mitigation Applied**:
- Searched for "Agricultural Bank of China CDM" without geographic restriction
- No evidence of international subsidiary CDM adoption found

**Residual Risk**: Medium - international subsidiaries less visible than parent

---

### 3. Chinese Language Evidence (HIGH RISK)

**Failure Mode**: CDM-relevant evidence exists in Chinese but not found via English searches

**Mitigation Applied**:
- Searched English-language sources which typically cover major Chinese banking initiatives
- NAFMII framework insight came from English-language trade press

**Residual Risk**: High - significant evidence could exist in Chinese-only sources

---

### 4. ISDA Membership Misinterpretation (MEDIUM RISK)

**Failure Mode**: Treating ISDA membership as evidence of no CDM involvement, when membership could precede adoption

**Mitigation Applied**:
- Explicitly noted membership ≠ adoption in evidence analysis
- Searched for CDM-specific evidence beyond membership claims

**Residual Risk**: Medium - future adoption possible via ISDA membership pathway

---

### 5. Regulatory Horizon Blindness (MEDIUM RISK)

**Failure Mode**: Missing that ISDA DRR Hong Kong (2025) could trigger ABC Hong Kong adoption

**Mitigation Applied**:
- Noted in cohort analysis that Hong Kong subsidiary could trigger future adoption
- Current classification is point-in-time, not permanent

**Residual Risk**: Medium - classification may need update in 2025-2026

---

## Highest Risk Factors

1. **Chinese language evidence gap** - could miss domestic announcements
2. **International subsidiary visibility** - Hong Kong/London operations less transparent
3. **Temporal risk** - ISDA DRR Hong Kong could trigger rapid change

---

## Pre-Mortem Conclusion

The research is robust within English-language evidence constraints. The cohort pattern is validated by independent ABC-specific evidence (ISDA membership claim). Primary failure mode is language barrier for Chinese domestic sources.
