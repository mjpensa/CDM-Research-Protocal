### 3. Does the ISDA membership change the assessment?

**No** - for the following reasons:

1. **Membership ≠ Adoption**: ISDA has 1,000+ members; most are not CDM adopters
2. **No CDM Evidence**: Despite claiming ISDA membership, no CDM-specific activity found
3. **NAFMII Still Applies**: Domestic derivatives still use NAFMII framework
4. **Structural Separation**: ISDA CDM does not cover Chinese regulatory reporting

The ISDA membership could be a future pathway to CDM adoption (especially via Hong Kong subsidiary for ISDA DRR) but does not indicate current adoption.
