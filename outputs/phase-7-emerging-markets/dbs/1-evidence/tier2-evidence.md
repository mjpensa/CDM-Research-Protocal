### DBS-E002: MAS Rewrite Regulatory Requirement

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Claim** | DBS Bank subject to MAS OTC Derivatives Reporting Rewrite effective October 21, 2024 |
| **Source** | [Reed Smith: Updated OTC Derivatives Reporting Regime](https://www.reedsmith.com/en/perspectives/2024/06/updated-otc-derivatives-contract-reporting-regime-from-21-october-2024) |
| **Tier** | 2 |
| **Claim Type** | membership_or_participation |
| **Source Date** | 2024-06-01 |
| **Freshness** | Current |

**Excerpt**:
> A rewrite of the rules went live on 21 October 2024. MAS implemented its own rewrite of the reporting rules, aligning with ASIC Rewrite.

**Key Requirements**:
- Adoption of ISO 20022 XML format
- 134 reportable data fields
- UTI (Unique Transaction Identifier) requirements
- Re-reporting of outstanding contracts by April 2025

**Analysis**:
This is contextual evidence - all Singapore banks are subject to MAS Rewrite. As Singapore's largest bank by assets, DBS would be a significant participant in derivatives reporting. However, this does not confirm CDM-specific adoption.
