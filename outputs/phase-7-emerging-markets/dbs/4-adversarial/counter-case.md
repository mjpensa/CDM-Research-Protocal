## Counter-Case: DBS Could Be PRAGMATIST (Traditional)

### Argument
DBS's ISDA Board membership is governance participation, not CDM-specific engagement. Many ISDA Board members represent banks that have not adopted CDM. The appropriate classification may be PRAGMATIST (Traditional) - a bank using traditional approaches to regulatory reporting.

### Evidence Supporting Counter-Case
1. No CDM production or pilot confirmed
2. No FINOS membership or contribution
3. No CDM-specific job postings
4. Traditional derivatives operations hiring patterns

### Weakness of Counter-Case
ISDA Board membership is active industry engagement that places DBS above purely passive banks. The OBSERVER classification captures this distinction without overclaiming technical adoption.

---

## Counter-Case: DBS Could Be ARCHITECT (Active)

### Argument
The unverified search result mentioned DBS among 15 firms running DRR proof-of-concept. If true, DBS would be ARCHITECT (Active) with pilot_or_poc evidence.

### Evidence Supporting Counter-Case
1. Andrew Ng's derivatives expertise and ISDA Board role
2. DBS's strong digital banking reputation
3. MAS Rewrite regulatory pressure
4. ISDA DRR availability for Singapore

### Weakness of Counter-Case
The POC claim could not be verified from primary sources. Per protocol, unverified claims cannot drive classification. If POC is later confirmed, classification should be upgraded.

---

## Conclusion

Both counter-cases have merit, but neither is strong enough to change the classification. OBSERVER with 55% confidence appropriately reflects the uncertainty.
