## Steelman: DBS is Running DRR POC

### Thesis
A search result summary mentioned DBS among 15 firms running ISDA DRR proof-of-concept initiatives. This would make DBS ARCHITECT (Active) with pilot_or_poc classification.

### Supporting Arguments
1. ISDA Board membership provides insider access to DRR initiatives
2. DBS is known as a digital banking leader ("World's Best Bank")
3. MAS Rewrite (Oct 2024) creates strong regulatory incentive
4. ISDA DRR is available for Singapore jurisdiction
5. Andrew Ng's derivatives expertise positions DBS well for early adoption

### Counter-Arguments
1. Primary source for POC claim not verified
2. Protocol requires verified evidence for classification
3. If POC were real, would expect more public visibility
4. Other Singapore banks not mentioned in POC list

### Assessment
**MODERATE** (25%) - The POC claim is plausible given DBS's profile, but cannot drive classification without verification. Recommend re-search in 6 months to check for POC confirmation.

## Steelman: DBS is PRAGMATIST (Traditional)

### Thesis
ISDA Board membership is governance participation, not CDM adoption. DBS is using traditional regulatory reporting approaches.

### Supporting Arguments
1. No CDM production or pilot evidence
2. No FINOS involvement
3. Traditional hiring patterns in regulatory reporting
4. Focus on digital assets/crypto rather than CDM

### Counter-Arguments
1. Board membership shows active industry engagement
2. OBSERVER captures this distinction better
3. Digital banking excellence could accelerate future CDM adoption

### Assessment
**WEAK** (15%) - PRAGMATIST classification would undervalue DBS's ISDA Board engagement. OBSERVER better captures the current state.

---

## Verdict

Both steelman arguments have merit but do not overcome the evidence-based OBSERVER classification at 55% confidence.
