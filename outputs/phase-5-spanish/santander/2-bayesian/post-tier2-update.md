## Likelihood Analysis

**L(No Tier 2 Evidence|H):** Likelihood of finding NO Tier 2 evidence IF bank has adopted CDM

  - CDM implementations at major banks typically receive trade press coverage
  - Vendor partnerships are usually announced publicly
  - Complete absence of Tier 2 signals strongly suggests no adoption

**L(No Tier 2 Evidence|¬H):** Likelihood of finding NO Tier 2 evidence IF bank has NOT adopted CDM

  - Consistent with "historical pilot only" hypothesis

---

## Bayesian Calculation

**Bayes Factor = L(E|H) / L(E|¬H) = 0.15 / 0.95 = 0.158**

**Posterior Odds = Prior Odds × Bayes Factor**

Prior Odds = 0.091 / 0.909 = 0.100
Posterior Odds = 0.100 × 0.158 = 0.0158

**P(Adoption | Tier 1 + Tier 2) = 0.0158 / (1 + 0.0158) = 1.6%**
