## Purpose

Present the **strongest possible argument** against the proposed classification. What is the best case that Santander is more CDM-engaged than assessed?

---

## Steelman Argument: Santander is Building CDM Capability Quietly

### Thesis

Santander is actively developing CDM capability but has not publicized this work. The 2022 DRR pilot was the beginning of a sustained but private initiative that will emerge publicly in 2025-2026.

---

### Supporting Arguments

#### 1. Strategic Timing Alignment

| Factor | Argument |
|--------|----------|
| EMIR Refit (April 2024) | Created compliance deadline requiring action |
| CFTC Rewrite (2025) | US operations need reporting modernization |
| JSCC CDM production (June 2025) | Asia connectivity may require CDM capability |
| Peer announcements | JPMorgan (Oct 2024) validates CDM investment |

**Steelman**: Santander may be timing their announcement for 2025 when regulatory deadlines converge and peer validation exists.

#### 2. DRR Pilot as Foundation

| Observation | Steelman Interpretation |
|-------------|-------------------------|
| 2022 pilot participation | Technical teams gained CDM expertise |
| No public follow-through | Internal development phase |
| Peer cohort success | Learned from Barclays, HSBC approaches |

**Steelman**: The DRR pilot provided a foundation for internal capability building that continued privately.

#### 3. CFTC Enforcement as Catalyst

| Observation | Steelman Interpretation |
|-------------|-------------------------|
| September 2024 enforcement | Highlighted reporting infrastructure gaps |
| Ongoing violations (2021-present) | Creates urgency for modernization |
| Remediation required | May drive CDM investment as solution |

**Steelman**: The enforcement action may have accelerated CDM investment as a remediation strategy. Banks often invest in technology after enforcement actions.

#### 4. Continental European Communication Norms

| Observation | Steelman Interpretation |
|-------------|-------------------------|
| No public announcements | Spanish banks are less disclosure-oriented |
| No LinkedIn advocacy | Corporate communications policy |
| No conference presentations | Waiting for production readiness |

**Steelman**: Unlike Anglo-American banks, Spanish institutions don't publicize technology strategies until production-ready.

## Counter-Steelman

The steelman argument is **plausible but not probable** because:

1. **3+ years is too long for hidden development**: Even private builds show hiring signals
2. **CFTC enforcement contradicts capability**: Organizations building CDM would improve recordkeeping
3. **Retail bank business model**: Santander's focus is on consumer banking, not derivatives infrastructure
4. **No FINOS engagement**: Even quiet builders engage with open source community
5. **Peer comparison**: Other DRR pilot participants (HSBC, NatWest) have visible CDM paths

---

## Verdict

The steelman argument has some merit but is **insufficiently supported** by evidence:

- **Probability if steelman correct**: Would upgrade to PRAGMATIST (Traditional) with active build, potentially ARCHITECT
- **Assessed probability of steelman**: 10-15%
- **Impact on classification**: Minimal - steelman probability too low to change classification

**Recommendation**: Maintain PRAGMATIST (Ecosystem) but note the possibility that 2025 may reveal hidden activity.
