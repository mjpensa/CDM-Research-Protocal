## Decision Framework

### Question 1: What is the protocol's intent?

**Analysis:**

The protocol's inclusion of DRR in scope suggests the designers view it as relevant to CDM adoption assessment. However, temporal thresholds exist to prevent outdated evidence from driving classifications.

**Interpretation:**

The protocol likely intends:
- DRR evidence IS relevant (when fresh)
- Historical DRR evidence provides context but cannot alone justify classification
- When ONLY historical evidence exists → UNKNOWN is appropriate

**Implication:** Position B (UNKNOWN) aligns better with protocol intent

---

### Question 2: What serves research users best?

**User Perspective:**

A research user wants to know: "What is Santander's current CDM posture?"

**Position A Answer:** "Historically engaged via DRR pilot, no current activity"
**Position B Answer:** "Insufficient evidence to determine current posture (historical DRR pilot noted)"

**Analysis:**

Both provide the key information (historical pilot, no current activity). Position B is more honest about uncertainty, while Position A forces a categorization that may misrepresent current state.

**Implication:** Position B (UNKNOWN) is more intellectually honest

---

### Question 3: What would a superforecaster do?

**Superforecasting Principles:**

Per Tetlock research (cited in CLAUDE.md Section 6):
1. Update beliefs based on evidence quality and recency
2. Admit uncertainty when evidence is limited
3. Avoid overconfidence in classifications
4. Distinguish between "know" and "suspect"

**Application:**

A superforecaster would:
- Note the historical DRR evidence (relevant signal)
- Heavily discount for 6+ year age (0.3 multiplier)
- Recognize DRR ≠ CDM (related but distinct)
- Conclude: Insufficient evidence to classify current posture
- Assign: UNKNOWN with detailed context

**Implication:** Position B (UNKNOWN) aligns with superforecasting approach

## Detailed Rationale

### Why UNKNOWN:

1. **Temporal Invalidity:**
   - All evidence >6 years old
   - Protocol: "Historical evidence cannot drive classification alone"
   - No recent corroboration to validate continued relevance

2. **Scope Uncertainty:**
   - DRR pilot is in-scope but not ISDA CDM-specific
   - Relationship between DRR and CDM unclear
   - Pilot predated widespread CDM adoption

3. **Substantiveness Unknown:**
   - Participation level not documented
   - No evidence of strategic engagement
   - Could have been minimal regulatory compliance

4. **Current State Unknown:**
   - Zero evidence of current CDM awareness
   - Bayesian probability 0.5% (effectively zero)
   - No signals of ongoing observation or monitoring

5. **Epistemic Honesty:**
   - Evidence insufficient to determine current posture
   - UNKNOWN admits limits rather than forcing classification
   - Serves users better than false precision

### Why Not OBSERVER:

1. OBSERVER implies current awareness (not supported)
2. Claim type is `pilot_or_poc` (not `membership_or_participation`)
3. Evidence violates temporal threshold for classification
4. Stretches category beyond design intent

### Why Confidence = 40%:

- HIGH confidence in facts (95%): Pilot occurred, no recent activity
- MODERATE uncertainty (40%) whether "insufficient evidence" is right label
- Could argue DRR in-scope justifies weak OBSERVER (35% confidence)
- Weighted assessment: 40% confidence in UNKNOWN as appropriate classification
