# Steelman Analysis: BBVA

**Bank**: Banco Bilbao Vizcaya Argentaria S.A.
**Phase**: 5 (Spanish)
**Date**: 2025-12-21
**Proposed Classification**: PRAGMATIST (Traditional)

---

## Purpose

Present the strongest argument against the proposed classification.

---

## Steelman Argument: BBVA Should Be OBSERVER

### Thesis

BBVA should be classified as OBSERVER rather than PRAGMATIST because there is **zero CDM evidence** and the derivatives activity does not indicate CDM relevance.

### Supporting Arguments

1. **Zero CDM signals**: Unlike peer Santander (DRR pilot), BBVA has no CDM-related evidence
2. **Retail bank business model**: BBVA's primary focus is retail banking in Spain and Latin America
3. **Swap dealer status is not CDM-relevant**: Many banks are swap dealers without any CDM involvement
4. **Murex/Calypso is common**: Most derivatives-active banks use these platforms; it proves nothing about CDM

### Quantitative Support

|------------|-----------|------|
| DRR Pilot | ✅ Yes | ❌ No |
| CDM Evidence Tier | 2 | None |
| Confidence | 55% | 45% |
| Clear Classification | Yes | No |

## Counter-Steelman

The steelman is **strong** but PRAGMATIST (Traditional) remains preferable because:

1. **OBSERVER implies minimal derivatives**: BBVA has active swap dealer operations
2. **Traditional captures no-CDM approach**: This subtype exists precisely for derivatives-active banks without CDM
3. **Classification preserves information**: Distinguishes BBVA from banks with truly minimal derivatives

---

## Impact

The steelman argument increases uncertainty:

| Classification | Pre-Steelman | Post-Steelman |
|---------------|--------------|---------------|
| PRAGMATIST | 48% | 45% |
| OBSERVER | 47% | 50% |

The margin is now within error. Classification is effectively a **coin flip**.

2. Document OBSERVER as equally valid alternative
3. Apply minimum viable confidence (45%)
4. Flag for re-evaluation with new evidence
