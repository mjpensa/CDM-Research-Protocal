## Decision Framework

### Question 1: What does the protocol intend?

**Analysis:**

CLAUDE.md prioritizes "Evidence-First" with "No inference without citation." However, it also recognizes Tier 4 inference in Synthesis stage:

> "Tier 4 represents logical deductions made from the absence or pattern of evidence."

**Interpretation:**

The protocol permits inference in Synthesis, but only AFTER evidence analysis is complete. We haven't reached Synthesis yet. At Adversarial stage, we should classify based on available evidence.

**Implication:** Position A (UNKNOWN) is more protocol-compliant

## Detailed Rationale

### Why UNKNOWN (With Confidence Bump to 35%):

1. **Evidence Requirements Not Met:**
   - PRAGMATIST requires evidence of vendor relationship or compliance strategy
   - We have neither direct evidence
   - Inference from regulatory necessity is insufficient

2. **Protocol Compliance:**
   - Evidence-first principle requires avoiding inference at classification stage
   - Inference is permitted in Synthesis (later stage), not Adversarial
   - Classification should precede interpretation

3. **But Confidence Increase (30% → 35%):**
   - Eight comprehensive null results ARE informative
   - Search methodology was solid
   - Acknowledges value of absence signals
   - Slight increase reflects confidence in evidence comprehensiveness
   - Does not rise to classification certainty

4. **Caveat for Synthesis:**
   - Assessment should note: "Null evidence suggests traditional compliance approach (PRAGMATIST likely), but insufficient direct evidence prevents classification"
   - This preserves evidence-first principle while acknowledging inference value
   - Defers inference to Synthesis stage (appropriate)

### Why Not PRAGMATIST:

1. **Insufficient Direct Evidence:**
   - No DTCC relationship documented
   - No vendor partnership documented
   - No compliance statement documented

2. **Inference from Absence:**
   - Violates "evidence-first" mandate at classification stage
   - Regulatory necessity ≠ evidence of vendor choice
   - BBVA could be using undisclosed internal solution

3. **Confidence Would Be Low:**
   - Even advocates suggest only 40-45%
   - Classification with 40% confidence is weak
   - Better to admit UNKNOWN than force weak classification
