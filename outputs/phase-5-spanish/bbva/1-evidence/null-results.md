## Alternative Explanations for Null Results

### Hypothesis 1: Stealth Implementation

**Claim:** BBVA is implementing CDM internally without public disclosure.

**Counter-Evidence:**
- No vendor partnerships (would be announced)
- No trade press leaks (journalists monitor major banks)
- No hiring signals (would require talent acquisition)
- **Likelihood:** <5%

### Hypothesis 2: Recent Initiative (Not Yet Public)

**Claim:** BBVA started CDM project very recently (last 3-6 months).

**Counter-Evidence:**
- No job postings (projects start with hiring)
- No working group participation (banks join early)
- No pilot participation (historical record)
- **Likelihood:** <10%

### Hypothesis 3: No CDM Program

**Claim:** BBVA is not pursuing CDM adoption.

**Supporting Evidence:**
- Comprehensive null results across all tiers
- No signals across 5+ year search window
- Standard derivatives operations possible without CDM
- **Likelihood:** >85%

**Conclusion:** Hypothesis 3 (No CDM Program) is most consistent with evidence.
