## Critical Null Result: UK DRR Pilot

### Search Finding
The UK DRR pilot (2018-2019, Phase 2 in 2022) included: **Barclays, Credit Suisse, HSBC, Lloyds, Nationwide, NatWest, and Santander**.

**BBVA was NOT a participant.**

### Implication
Unlike Spanish peer Santander (who participated in DRR pilot Phase 2), BBVA has **no demonstrated CDM ecosystem engagement**. This is the key differentiator between the two Spanish banks.
