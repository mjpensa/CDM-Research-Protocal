  - Vendor partnerships are always announced

## Bayesian Calculation

**Bayes Factor = L(E|H) / L(E|¬H) = 0.10 / 0.98 = 0.102**

**Posterior Odds = Prior Odds × Bayes Factor**

Prior Odds = 0.009 / 0.991 = 0.00908
Posterior Odds = 0.00908 × 0.102 = 0.00093

**P(Adoption | Tier 1 + Tier 2) = 0.00093 / (1 + 0.00093) = 0.09%**
