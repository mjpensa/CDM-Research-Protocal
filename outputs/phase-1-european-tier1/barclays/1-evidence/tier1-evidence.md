# Tier 1 Evidence: Barclays PLC

## Executive Summary

Barclays demonstrates **strong CDM advocacy** led by Lee Braine, with significant participation in industry initiatives including hackathons, pilots, and research publications. However, **no evidence of production deployment** was found. Barclays appears to be a **prominent Follower** rather than a Leader.

---

## Evidence Classification

### Category 1: Direct CDM Involvement

#### 1.1 DerivHack Hackathons (Organizer/Host)
- **DerivHack 2018**: Barclays hosted this hackathon at Rise London and New York
  - Co-sponsored by ISDA, Deloitte, Thomson Reuters
  - 31 teams, 140 coders participated
  - Objective: Implement ISDA CDM through emerging technology
  - Source: [Ledger Insights](https://www.ledgerinsights.com/barclays-blockchain-hackathon-derivatives/)

- **DerivHack 2019**: Second iteration of the industry hackathon
  - Collaboration with ICMA, ISLA, ISDA, FINOS
  - Source: [FINOS](https://www.finos.org)

- **Repo Hackathon 2023**: September 27-28, London
  - Used FINOS CDM and Barclays-provided APIs
  - Focus: Post-trade processing for repo transactions
  - Collaboration with ICMA, ISLA, ISDA, FINOS
  - Source: [Finadium](https://finadium.com/barclays-announces-repo-hackathon-using-cdm/)

**Assessment**: Strong ecosystem leadership in **education/demonstration**, but hackathons are not production deployments.

#### 1.2 UK DRR Pilot Participation
- **Phase 2 (2018-2019)**: Barclays confirmed participant
  - Alongside Credit Suisse, HSBC, NatWest, Santander, Lloyds
  - FCA and Bank of England led pilot
  - ISDA CDM 2.0 deployed for testing
  - Source: [Regulation Asia](https://www.regulationasia.com/isda-cdm-deployed-in-uk-digital-regulatory-reporting-pilot/)

**Assessment**: **Pilot participation** - not production. Status from 2018-2019, no confirmation of production follow-through.

#### 1.3 Internal CDM Working Group
- Barclays set up internal CDM adoption working group
- Projected 25% efficiency gains from CDM in clearing space
- Estimated $2.5 billion annual cost savings potential
- Source: [Ledger Insights](https://www.ledgerinsights.com/barclays-blockchain-hackathon-derivatives/)

**Assessment**: Internal exploration, no production deployment confirmation.

### Category 2: Lee Braine Activity

#### 2.1 Role and Position
- **Title**: Managing Director, Chief Technology Office, Barclays
- **Focus Areas**: CDM, blockchain/DLT, CBDC, post-trade infrastructure
- **Bank of England**: Member of CBDC Technology Forum for digital pound

#### 2.2 CDM-Specific Statements
- "50-70% efficiency increase possible by restructuring data for interest rate swaps to use CDM" (CDM Showcase, February 2023)
- "Use CDM or some business lines might die" - warned post-trade expenses could threaten business viability
- "We store an interest rate swap 24 times across the industry"
- Predicted market infrastructure providers will deploy CDM first, then banks, then regulators
- Sources: [Risk.net](https://www.risk.net/derivatives/7147901/use-cdm-or-some-business-lines-might-die-barclays), [BobsGuide](https://www.bobsguide.com/barclays-cto-isdas-cdm-to-drive-trading-efficiency/)

#### 2.3 Research Publications
- "Simulation of Derivatives Post-Trade Services using an Authoritative Data Store and the ISDA Common Domain Model" (2021) - with Vikram A. Bakshi, Aishwarya Nair
- "Industry Adoption Scenarios for Authoritative Data Stores using the ISDA Common Domain Model" (2020)
- Sources: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.02571), [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.06507)

#### 2.4 2024 Activity Shift
- 2024 publications focused on **CBDC** rather than CDM:
  - "Payments Use Cases and Design Options for Interoperability and Funds Locking across Digital Pounds and Commercial Bank Money" (2024)
  - Digital pound paper questioning need for retail CBDC (September 2024)
  - Source: [Ledger Insights](https://www.ledgerinsights.com/barclays-paper-questions-need-for-digital-pound-to-preserve-singleness-of-money/)

**Assessment**: Lee Braine is a **prominent advocate** but his 2024 focus has shifted toward CBDC. No evidence his advocacy has translated to production CDM deployment at Barclays.

## Key Findings

### Positive Evidence (Follower Confirmation)
1. Barclays has organized **three major CDM hackathons** (2018, 2019, 2023)
2. Participated in **UK DRR pilot** (2018-2019)
3. **Lee Braine** is a prominent CDM advocate with research publications
4. Internal **CDM working group** established
5. Published **white papers** on CDM adoption scenarios
6. Active in **FINOS community** as participant

### Negative Evidence (Leader Criteria Not Met)
1. **No production deployment** found
2. **No governance/steering roles** at ISDA CDM or FINOS CDM
3. **No maintainer role** in CDM project
4. **No UK EMIR CDM-based compliance** announced
5. Lee Braine's 2024 focus shifted to **CBDC** from CDM
6. Barclays' own timeline estimate: production "**5-10 years away**"
