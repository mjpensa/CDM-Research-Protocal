# Null Results Documentation: Barclays PLC

## Overview

This document records searches that returned no evidence, which are analytically significant for the hypothesis being tested: "Barclays has upgraded from ARCHITECT-Follower to ARCHITECT-Leader."

---

## Critical Null Results

### 1. Production Deployment Evidence

**Search**: `"Barclays" CDM production deployment implementation 2023 2024`

**Result**: NULL - No evidence found

**Significance**: HIGH - This is the primary Leader criterion. Absence of production deployment evidence directly supports Follower classification.

**Alternative Interpretations**:
- Production may be confidential/unannounced
- Production may be under different terminology
- Production may be imminent but not yet deployed

**Counter-search attempted**: `"Barclays" derivatives technology production pilot CDM` - Also null for production

---

### 2. CDM Governance Role

## Null Results Pattern Analysis

### Theme 1: Advocacy Without Formalization
- High visibility (hackathons, talks, papers)
- Low formal roles (no maintainer, no steering)
- Suggests influence through thought leadership rather than governance

### Theme 2: Pilot Without Production
- UK DRR pilot (2018-2019) confirmed
- No production follow-through found (6+ years later)
- Internal working group but no deployment announcement

### Theme 3: Champion Individual, Cautious Institution
- Lee Braine highly visible and published
- Barclays corporate silent on CDM strategy
- Gap between personal advocacy and institutional commitment
