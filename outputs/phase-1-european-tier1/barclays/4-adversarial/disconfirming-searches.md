# Disconfirming Evidence Searches: Barclays PLC

## Purpose
This document records searches specifically designed to find evidence that would **disconfirm** the primary classification of ARCHITECT-Follower.

---

## Disconfirming Search Strategy

To challenge the Follower classification, we need evidence of:
1. Production CDM deployment
2. CDM governance/leadership roles
3. Technical maintainer contributions
4. Regulatory CDM-based compliance

---

## Search Results

### Search 1: Production Deployment
**Query**: `Barclays CDM implementation live production 2024 derivatives`

**Result**: NO DISCONFIRMING EVIDENCE
- Found references to hackathons (not production)
- Found advocacy statements (not deployment)
- Found "patchy take-up" observation by Barclays itself
- Barclays paper advocates FMIs should lead (not self-deployment)

**Key Quote Found**: "CDM has seen patchy take-up among banks and financial market infrastructures since it was conceived in 2017"
- This statement from Barclays itself suggests they are observing, not deploying

---

### Search 2: Governance Roles
**Query**: `"Lee Braine" ISDA FINOS board committee 2024`

**Result**: NO DISCONFIRMING EVIDENCE
- ISDA Board additions in 2024: Did not include Lee Braine or any Barclays representative
- FINOS Governing Board: No current Barclays representative identified
- CDM Steering Working Group: No Barclays participant identified as chair/co-chair

**Note**: FINOS CDM Collateral Working Group (September 2024) and Derivatives Working Group (September 2024) exist, but no Barclays leadership role identified.

---

### Search 3: RegTech/CDM Integration
**Query**: `Barclays RegTech regulatory technology CDM 2024`

**Result**: PARTIAL EVIDENCE (but not disconfirming)
- Barclays mentioned as G-SIB that has "piloted various projects related to trade and transaction reporting"
- Barclays described as using RegTech for AML monitoring (not CDM-related)
- No specific CDM-based regulatory compliance announced

**Key Finding**: Article states "Goldman Sachs, JP Morgan, Barclays and BNP Paribas have all piloted various projects" - note: **piloted**, not **deployed in production**

---

### Search 4: CDM Production Timeline
**Query**: Risk.net article on CDM needs infrastructure push

**Result**: CONFIRMS FOLLOWER STATUS
- Barclays advocates market infrastructures (FMIs, CCPs) should drive CDM adoption
- Explicitly positions banks as **followers** of FMI adoption
- No timeline for Barclays own production deployment mentioned
- Acknowledges banks "struggle" with CDM adoption business case

**Key Quote**: "The business-case challenge is not to persuade all the broker-dealers to migrate off existing functioning internal systems. It is merely to add new services"
- This indicates no current migration is occurring at Barclays

---

### Search 5: Lee Braine 2024 Focus
**Query**: `"Lee Braine" Barclays 2024 blockchain derivatives CBDC`

**Result**: CONFIRMS FOLLOWER STATUS
- Lee Braine's 2024 publications focused on **CBDC**, not CDM
- September 2024: Digital pound paper
- September 2024: Programmable CBDC paper
- No 2024 CDM-specific publications found

**Interpretation**: If Barclays were pushing CDM to production, their chief advocate would be driving CDM implementation, not CBDC research. Focus shift suggests CDM is not immediate priority.
