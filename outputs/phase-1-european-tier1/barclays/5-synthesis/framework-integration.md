## 9. Framework Lessons from Barclays Case

### Lesson 1: Advocacy =/= Adoption
High visibility through hackathons, research, and speaking does not indicate production commitment. Framework must distinguish these.

### Lesson 2: Individual Champions =/= Institutional Commitment
Lee Braine's prominence does not equal Barclays' production commitment. Framework should not conflate individual and institutional signals.

### Lesson 3: Time Decay Matters
6+ years from pilot to no production is definitive evidence. Framework should weight recency and progression.

### Lesson 4: Self-Identification Is Strong Evidence
Barclays' "FMI-first" strategy is explicit Follower positioning. Framework should capture self-identifying statements.

### Lesson 5: Regulatory Triggers Are Tests
UK EMIR September 2024 was a natural adoption trigger. Non-response is meaningful evidence.
