### Search 2: "HSBC" CDM derivatives technology

**Result**: POSITIVE - UK DRR PILOT PARTICIPATION CONFIRMED

Key findings from [Regulation Asia](https://www.regulationasia.com/isda-cdm-deployed-in-uk-digital-regulatory-reporting-pilot/):
- HSBC participated in UK Digital Regulatory Reporting pilot alongside FCA, Bank of England, Barclays, Credit Suisse, NatWest, Santander, and Lloyds
- ISDA deployed CDM 2.0 for phase two of DRR pilot (2019)
- Purpose: Test feasibility of position-based and transaction-based reporting from same trade data

Key findings from [A-Team Insight](https://a-teaminsight.com/blog/delta-capitas-elaris-otc-stitches-new-rails-into-the-derivatives-backoffice/?brand=ati):
- Delta Capita launched Elaris OTC - cloud-native OTC derivatives confirmation platform
- Service translates all messages into ISDA CDM
- HSBC mandate provides built-in volume for platform

**Evidence Type**: Historical participation (DRR pilot), Current vendor relationship (Delta Capita)
**Bayesian Impact**: Moderate positive for ARCHITECT (pilot participation), requires clarification on vendor scope

---

### Search 3: "HSBC" "Delta Capita" CDM derivatives

**Result**: STRONG EVIDENCE - MULTI-YEAR GLOBAL OUTSOURCING AGREEMENT

Key findings from [Delta Capita Official Press Release](https://www.deltacapita.com/insights/delta-capita-secures-multi-year-agreement-to-deliver-post-trade-otc-derivative-services-for-hsbc):

**Agreement Details**:
- January 2025 multi-year agreement
- Delta Capita to deliver OTC derivatives confirmation and settlement services **globally** for HSBC
- Quote from Karen Everingham (HSBC Global Head of Markets and Securities Services Operations): "The agreement we have established with Delta Capita opens up new opportunities for us to enhance our Derivative Post-Trade Services for our valued clients."

**Scope Assessment** (Critical for Classification):
- Confirmation services
- Settlement services
- Global coverage
- This appears to be **comprehensive outsourcing**, not component-based

From [The Trade News](https://www.thetradenews.com/delta-capita-to-deliver-post-trade-otc-derivative-services-for-hsbc/):
- Delta Capita selected by HSBC for global OTC derivative services
- Elaris OTC platform uses CDM standard for interoperability

**Evidence Type**: Vendor contract announcement
**Bayesian Impact**: Strong signal for PRAGMATIST (Vendor-dependent) - outsourcing appears comprehensive

**Result**: GENERAL TECHNOLOGY INVESTMENT, NO CDM-SPECIFIC EVIDENCE

**Evidence Type**: Technology strategy direction

## Key Evidence Gaps

1. **No evidence of internal CDM capability development at HSBC**
   - No CDM teams, projects, or systems mentioned
   - No hiring for CDM-related roles found

2. **No evidence of sustained post-2018 DRR pilot engagement**
   - Pilot participation confirmed
   - No follow-through visible

3. **No evidence of HSBC contributing to CDM standards development**
   - ISDA board presence is governance role
   - No technical contributions to CDM specification

4. **Delta Capita scope appears comprehensive**
   - Global coverage
   - Confirmation AND settlement services
   - Suggests complete outsourcing, not component use

---

## Preliminary Classification Signal

**Based on Tier 1 Evidence**: PRAGMATIST (Vendor-dependent)

**Rationale**:
1. Delta Capita provides comprehensive global CDM capability
2. HSBC has no visible internal CDM development
3. Broader outsourcing strategy (trading operations to market makers)
4. 2018 DRR participation did not lead to internal capability
5. ISDA engagement is governance-level, not technical contribution
