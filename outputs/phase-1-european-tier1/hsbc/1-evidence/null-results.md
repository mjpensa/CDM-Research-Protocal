# Null Results Documentation: HSBC Holdings PLC

## Research Date
2025-12-19

## Purpose
This document records searches that returned negative or null results. Per research protocol, null results are evidentially significant - the absence of expected evidence for an ARCHITECT classification is itself evidence supporting PRAGMATIST classification.

---

## Search Null Results

### 1. HSBC.com CDM Content

**Search**: "HSBC" "Common Domain Model" site:hsbc.com

**Expected Finding (if ARCHITECT)**:
- Public documentation of CDM initiatives
- CDM-related content in technology or innovation sections
- Press releases about CDM adoption or development

**Actual Result**:
- No CDM content found
- "Common Reporting Standard" pages returned (tax reporting, unrelated)
- General financial regulations pages without CDM mention

**Significance**:
- ARCHITECT banks typically publicize their CDM engagement (e.g., Goldman Sachs, Morgan Stanley CDM hackathons)
- Absence suggests CDM is not part of HSBC's public technology positioning
- Supports PRAGMATIST classification

## Evidential Weight of Null Results

**Cumulative Assessment**: The pattern of null results is highly significant.

For a bank of HSBC's size and derivatives market presence, if it were building internal CDM capability (ARCHITECT), we would expect to find:
1. Public positioning around CDM
2. Hiring for CDM roles
3. Contributions to standards bodies
4. Post-pilot implementation evidence
5. Internal technology documentation

**None of these were found.**

Combined with positive evidence of:
- Comprehensive outsourcing to Delta Capita (CDM-native vendor)
- Broader strategy of outsourcing trading operations
- Gap between 2018 pilot and 2025 vendor contract

**Conclusion**: Null results strongly support PRAGMATIST (Vendor-dependent) classification.
