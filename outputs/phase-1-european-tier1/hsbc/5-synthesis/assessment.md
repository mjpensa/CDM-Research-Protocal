## Section 2: Classification Justification

### Primary Classification: PRAGMATIST (Vendor-dependent)

**Definition**: PRAGMATIST (Vendor-dependent) banks do not build internal CDM capability. They rely entirely on vendors for CDM connectivity and compliance.

**HSBC Classification Rationale**:

1. **Comprehensive Vendor Outsourcing**: Delta Capita provides global OTC derivatives confirmation and settlement services using CDM-native technology (Fragmos Chain)

2. **No Internal CDM Development**: Despite extensive research, no evidence of internal CDM teams, projects, or technology investments

3. **Gap Without Implementation**: 6 years between DRR pilot participation (2018-2019) and Delta Capita outsourcing (2025) with no visible internal CDM activity

4. **Consistent Strategic Pattern**: Broader HSBC strategy includes outsourcing trading operations to reduce IT costs, consistent with PRAGMATIST approach

### Why NOT ARCHITECT

ARCHITECT classification requires:
- Internal CDM capability development
- Strategic vendor partnership (not outsourcing)
- Contribution to CDM ecosystem

HSBC fails all three criteria:
- No internal CDM capability found
- Delta Capita is outsourcing, not strategic partnership
- No CDM contributions to ISDA or FINOS working groups

## Section 5: Delta Capita Scope Assessment

### Scope Determination: COMPREHENSIVE (Full Outsourcing)

**Evidence from Announcement (January 28, 2025)**:
- "deliver OTC derivatives confirmation and settlement services globally"
- Multi-year agreement duration
- Both confirmation AND settlement covered
- Global geographic scope

**Technology Stack**:

**Assessment**: HSBC is not using Delta Capita as a component within a larger internal CDM strategy. Delta Capita provides the complete CDM solution for OTC derivatives post-trade.

- 2018-2019: Pilot participation

**Conclusion**: Pilot participation was exploratory. Did not lead to internal capability building.
