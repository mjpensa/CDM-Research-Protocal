### Argument 1: ISDA Board Chair
**Claim**: Having the ISDA Board Chair indicates deep derivatives standardization commitment.

**Counter-Evidence**:

- HSBC has been on ISDA Board since 2016
- Board role shapes CDM/DRR industry direction

**Rebuttal**: Board Chair is governance/policy role. It indicates influence over standards but not technical adoption. Many ISDA Board members are from non-CDM-adopting firms.
