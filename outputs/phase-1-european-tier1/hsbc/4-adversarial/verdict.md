## Classification Rationale

HSBC is classified as **PRAGMATIST (Vendor-dependent)** because:

### 1. CDM Capability Resides Externally
HSBC's CDM capability is provided entirely by Delta Capita/Fragmos Chain. There is no evidence of internal CDM development, teams, or technology.

### 2. Outsourcing is Comprehensive, Not Component-Based
The Delta Capita agreement covers global OTC derivatives confirmation AND settlement services. This is not using vendor as component; it's complete outsourcing of post-trade CDM functionality.

### 3. Pattern Shows No Internal Investment
The 6-year gap between DRR pilot (2019) and Delta Capita outsourcing (2025), combined with null results for internal CDM activity, demonstrates that HSBC did not invest in building internal CDM capability.

### 4. Industry Engagement Without Technical Contribution
HSBC engaged at industry governance level (ISDA Board) but did not contribute to CDM technical development. This is characteristic of PRAGMATIST: participate for market presence without internal capability building.

### 5. Strategic Direction is Outsourcing, Not Building
HSBC's broader strategy includes outsourcing trading operations to reduce IT costs. CDM outsourcing via Delta Capita is consistent with this approach.
