## Key Assessment Questions

### 1. Is there evidence of INTERNAL CDM capability building beyond vendor?

**Answer**: NO

**Evidence**:
- No internal CDM teams or projects found
- No CDM-related hiring
- No technology investments in CDM
- CDM capability entirely via Delta Capita/Fragmos Chain

**Conclusion**: HSBC is not building internal CDM capability

---

### 2. What is the scope of the Delta Capita engagement?

**Answer**: COMPREHENSIVE (Full CDM Outsourcing)

**Evidence from Delta Capita announcement**:
- "deliver OTC derivatives confirmation and settlement services **globally**"
- Multi-year agreement
- Both confirmation AND settlement services
- Global coverage (not regional pilot)

**Conclusion**: This is not component-based vendor use; it's comprehensive outsourcing of CDM capability

---

### 3. Did 2018 pilot participation lead to sustained internal expertise?

**Answer**: NO

**Evidence**:
- 2018-2019: DRR pilot participation (collaborative)
- 2020-2024: No visible CDM activity or investment
- 2025: Outsourced CDM to Delta Capita

**Gap Analysis**:
- 6 years between pilot and outsourcing
- If pilot led to internal expertise, would expect internal implementation
- Instead, HSBC chose to outsource entirely

**Conclusion**: Pilot was exploratory participation, not foundation for internal capability

---

### 4. How do Asian regulatory requirements (HKMA, MAS) affect HSBC's approach?

**Answer**: MINIMAL CDM DRIVER

**Evidence**:
- HKMA/MAS have OTC derivatives reporting requirements
- These are aligning with ESMA standards
- No CDM mandate in Asian jurisdictions found
- HSBC's Asian operations not driving CDM adoption

**Conclusion**: Asian regulatory context is neutral to PRAGMATIST classification; no CDM requirement forcing internal development

---

### 5. Is the outsourcing strategic or purely compliance-driven?

**Answer**: COMPLIANCE/COST-DRIVEN

**Evidence**:
- Bloomberg: HSBC considering outsourcing trading to cut IT costs
- Delta Capita quote: "opens up new opportunities to enhance our Derivative Post-Trade Services for our valued clients"
- No strategic CDM positioning in announcement

**Assessment**:
- Language focuses on client services and operational efficiency
- No mention of strategic technology transformation
- Aligns with cost-reduction strategy

**Conclusion**: Delta Capita engagement is compliance/efficiency driven, not strategic CDM adoption
