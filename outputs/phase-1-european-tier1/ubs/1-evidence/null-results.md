## Null Result 1: UBS.com Direct CDM Reference

**Search Query**: `"UBS" "Common Domain Model" site:ubs.com`

**Actual Result**: No results containing "Common Domain Model" found on ubs.com

**Interpretation**:

**Impact on Assessment**: Minor negative signal, offset by strong external evidence

## Null Result 4: Swiss Regulatory CDM Requirements

**Search Query**: `"UBS" regulatory reporting CDM Switzerland`

**Expected Finding**: FINMA requirements driving CDM adoption in Switzerland

**Actual Result**: No CDM-specific regulatory requirements found

**Results Returned**: General UBS regulatory information, Basel III disclosures, FINMA supervision details - no CDM mandates

**Interpretation**:
- Switzerland/FINMA has not mandated CDM for derivatives reporting
- CDM adoption at UBS is voluntary/industry-driven, not regulatory-mandated
- Contrasts with UK (FCA DRR interest) and EU (EMIR evolution)

**Impact on Assessment**: Minor context - CDM adoption is strategic choice, not compliance requirement

## Implications for Assessment

### What Null Results Tell Us

1. **Swiss Discretion Confirmed**: UBS's public communications are conservative - external sources more revealing than corporate communications

2. **UBS-Native CDM Engagement**: Credit Suisse brought no CDM legacy; UBS's engagement is original

3. **No Regulatory Driver in Switzerland**: CDM adoption is strategic/industry-driven, not FINMA-mandated

4. **Implementation Opacity**: We can confirm participation but not production deployment

5. **Integration Focus Elsewhere**: Post-CS priorities do not include CDM acceleration

### Confidence Impact

Null results suggest:
- Strong evidence of participation confirmed
- Weak evidence of production implementation
- Appropriate confidence range: +/- 15%
