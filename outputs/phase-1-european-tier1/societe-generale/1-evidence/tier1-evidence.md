# Tier 1 Evidence: Societe Generale

**Bank**: Societe Generale
**Phase**: 1 (European Tier 1)
**Evidence Tier**: 1 (Public sources)
**Collection Date**: 2025-12-19
**Prior Probability**: P(Architect) = 45%

---

## Executive Summary

Tier 1 evidence gathering found **no direct CDM adoption evidence** for Societe Generale. However, significant contextual evidence was gathered regarding:
- Eric Litvack's 10-year ISDA chairmanship (2015-2024)
- Strong digital transformation and derivatives technology activity
- SG-FORGE digital asset innovation
- Capitolis partnership for derivatives automation
- EMIR Refit compliance awareness

**Key Finding**: SocGen is clearly derivatives-technology active but has no explicit CDM footprint in Tier 1 sources.

## Evidence Blocks

### Evidence Block 1

**Source**: ISDA Official - "ISDA Board Appoints New Chair as Eric Litvack Steps Down After 10 Years"
**URL**: https://www.isda.org/2024/12/16/isda-board-appoints-new-chair-as-eric-litvack-steps-down-after-10-years/
**Date**: December 16, 2024
**Source Type**: ISDA Official
**Tier**: 1

**Finding**: Eric Litvack, Managing Director and Group Director of Public Affairs at Societe Generale, served as ISDA Chairman from January 2015 until December 2024 - the longest-serving Chair in ISDA's 40-year history. "During his chairmanship, ISDA has taken a leading role in several critical regulatory and market reforms, including the transition from LIBOR and the development of robust contractual fallbacks based on risk-free rates, implementation of new margin rules for non-cleared derivatives and the creation of the ISDA Standard Initial Margin Model, and the shift to greater automation and digitization of derivatives markets."

**Interpretation**: SocGen had maximum influence at the industry body that governs CDM during the period when CDM was developed and launched. However, Litvack's stated priorities were LIBOR transition and margin rules, not specifically CDM. His departure in 2024 may reduce SocGen's ISDA influence going forward.

**Strength**: Strong
**Direction**: NEUTRAL (influence present, but CDM adoption not confirmed)

### Evidence Block 2

**Source**: ISDA AGM Agenda Page
**URL**: https://agm.isda.org/agenda/
**Date**: 2024/2025
**Source Type**: ISDA Official
**Tier**: 1

**Finding**: Eric Litvack from Societe Generale is listed as moderator for ISDA AGM sessions where "the potential application of the Common Domain Model (CDM) to facilitate reporting and compliance" was discussed alongside topics including digital assets, ESG transactions, and accounting standards.

**Interpretation**: This is the closest link found between SocGen personnel and CDM. However, moderating a panel discussing CDM does not indicate SocGen internal adoption. Litvack's role was industry governance, not necessarily reflecting firm-level implementation decisions.

**Strength**: Moderate
**Direction**: NEUTRAL-ARCHITECT (awareness confirmed, implementation unclear)

### Evidence Block 3

**Source**: ISDA Official - "Major Banks Agree to Sign ISDA Resolution Stay Protocol"
**URL**: https://www.isda.org/2014/10/11/major-banks-agree-to-sign-isda-resolution-stay-protocol/
**Date**: October 11, 2014
**Source Type**: ISDA Official
**Tier**: 1

**Interpretation**: Historical evidence of SocGen participating in ISDA-led standardization initiatives. Pattern suggests willingness to adopt industry standards when there is regulatory or systemic benefit. However, this predates CDM by several years.

**Strength**: Weak
**Direction**: NEUTRAL (historical pattern only)

### Evidence Block 4

**Source**: Societe Generale Wholesale Banking - EMIR Page
**URL**: https://wholesale.banking.societegenerale.com/en/compliance-regulatory-information/market-regulation/emir/
**Date**: Current (accessed 2025-12-19)
**Source Type**: Official Bank
**Tier**: 1

**Finding**: SocGen's official EMIR page states: "EU EMIR Refit revised transaction reporting standards applied from 29 April 2024" and "UK EMIR Refit revised reporting requirements applied from 30 September 2024." The page describes EMIR obligations including reporting to trade repositories, central clearing requirements, and risk mitigation techniques. No mention of implementation technology or CDM usage.

**Interpretation**: SocGen is clearly compliant with EMIR Refit (regulatory necessity). However, the absence of any technology methodology mention (no CDM, no DRR, no vendor reference) suggests either: (a) implementation details are not publicly disclosed, or (b) traditional/non-CDM implementation approach. Contrast with BNP Paribas which publicly announced DRR/CDM usage for CFTC rules.

**Strength**: Moderate
**Direction**: PRAGMATIST (compliance without CDM mention)

### Evidence Block 5

**Source**: SG-FORGE Official Website
**URL**: https://www.sgforge.com/
**Date**: Current (2025)
**Source Type**: Official Bank Subsidiary
**Tier**: 1

**Finding**: "SG-FORGE provides institutional issuers and investors with innovative services to issue and manage financial products on the blockchain." Established in 2019, SG-FORGE is "a fully integrated subsidiary of Societe Generale Group, regulated as an investment firm under EU MiFID2 Regulation, as an Electronic Money Institution under MiCA (Markets in Crypto-assets) Regulation and as a digital asset service provider under French law." Key products include EUR CoinVertible (EURCV) and USD CoinVertible (USDCV) stablecoins, plus tokenized bond issuance.

**Interpretation**: SocGen is clearly investing heavily in blockchain/DLT innovation for capital markets. However, SG-FORGE focuses on digital asset tokenization, not derivatives standardization or regulatory reporting. This represents an alternative innovation path that may compete for resources and strategic attention with CDM initiatives.

**Strength**: Strong
**Direction**: PRAGMATIST (alternative innovation focus)

### Evidence Block 9

**Source**: BNP Paribas CDM Production Reference (Comparative)
**URL**: https://www.isda.org/2022/11/02/isda-and-bnp-paribas-successfully-test-digital-regulatory-reporting-for-cftc-rules/
**Date**: November 2, 2022
**Source Type**: ISDA Official
**Tier**: 1

**Finding**: "ISDA announced today that BNP Paribas has successfully implemented and tested a new initiative to enable digital regulatory reporting under the US Commodity Futures Trading Commission's (CFTC) amended swap data reporting rules. This marks the first time ISDA's Digital Regulatory Reporting (DRR) initiative was deployed in a real-world, production-level environment." BNP Paribas stated they completed "converting internal trade and counterparty data to CDM format."

**Interpretation**: This is the benchmark for French Tier 1 CDM adoption. BNP Paribas publicly announced production CDM usage for CFTC rules in November 2022. The hypothesis suggests SocGen would follow with 12-24 month lag, meaning expected announcement window was November 2023 - November 2024. No such announcement has been found. This either means: (a) SocGen is not following BNP, (b) SocGen is implementing quietly without announcement, or (c) lag is longer than hypothesized.

**Strength**: Strong (as comparative reference)
**Direction**: NEUTRAL (benchmark only, no SocGen evidence)

SocGen appears to be pursuing AI, cloud, digital assets, and vendor partnerships rather than industry data standardization.
