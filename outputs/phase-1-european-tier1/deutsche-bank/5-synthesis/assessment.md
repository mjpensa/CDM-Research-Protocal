Deutsche Bank demonstrates that:

**Pattern Contribution**: FINOS governance roles should not be used as proxy for CDM production likelihood.

#### European Bank CDM Adoption Patterns

Deutsche Bank contributes to understanding that:

1. **European majors show varied CDM engagement**: BNP Paribas (production) vs Deutsche Bank (dormant) vs others
2. **EMIR Refit alone is insufficient driver**: April 2024 deadline did not push Deutsche Bank to CDM production
3. **German regulatory culture may differ**: BaFin supervision may not prioritize CDM adoption

**Pattern Contribution**: European Tier 1 banks should be assessed individually; regional generalizations are risky.
