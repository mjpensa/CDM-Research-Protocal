# Targeted Disconfirming Searches: Deutsche Bank AG

**Purpose**: Execute searches designed to find evidence that would OVERTURN the PRAGMATIST classification
**Date**: 2025-12-19

---

## Search 1: Internal CDM Work / Technology Roadmap Signals

**Query**: `"Deutsche Bank" CDM "Common Domain Model" technology roadmap internal derivatives platform modernization 2024 2025`

**Results Found**:
- No specific evidence of Deutsche Bank internal CDM adoption or technology roadmap
- General CDM industry information (ISDA, FINOS, ICMA resources)
- Deutsche Bank technology transformation announcements:
  - IBM partnership (May 2025) for "business and IT automation stack, advanced hybrid cloud products, watsonx AI portfolio"
  - Oracle partnership (2021) for database modernization including "trading, payments processing, risk and capital planning, and regulatory reporting"
  - GFT partnership for cloud migration of "business-critical applications"
- No mention of CDM in any Deutsche Bank technology announcements

**Impact on Counter-Case**:
**WEAKENS counter-case**. Despite extensive technology transformation activity, CDM is never mentioned. The IBM and Oracle partnerships could theoretically include CDM components, but there is no evidence supporting this interpretation. The absence of CDM in explicit derivatives/regulatory reporting modernization contexts is notable.

## Search 2: Recent FINOS CDM/DRR Activity

**Query**: `"Deutsche Bank" FINOS CDM Legend 2024 2025 digital regulatory reporting DRR pilot`

**Results Found**:

**Positive findings (FINOS involvement, but NOT CDM-specific)**:
- **Fluxnova (October 2025)**: Deutsche Bank is co-founding member alongside Fidelity, NatWest, Capital One
  - Quote from Stefan Schaffer (CEO Deutsche India): "Fluxnova provides us with a predictable long-term model that supports process automation and transparent workflow management. These are essential activities as we improve the client experience, safeguard compliance and strengthen resilience."
- **Waltz**: Deutsche Bank contributed enterprise architecture tool to FINOS
- Continued FINOS membership and participation

**Negative findings (CDM/DRR specifically)**:
- No mention of Deutsche Bank in CDM or DRR context in 2024-2025
- CDM/DRR production announcements mention other banks:
  - JPMorgan Chase: adopted CDM/DRR as primary reporting mechanism
  - Japan Exchange Group (JPX): CDM-based DRR in production parallel
  - JSCC: First CCP to announce DRR/CDM production adoption (June 2025)
  - Goldman Sachs, Barclays, BNP Paribas: piloted various CDM projects
- Deutsche Bank NOT mentioned among banks "harnessing open source innovation to transform regulatory reporting"

**Historical confirmation**:
- 2020 Legend pilot with CDM extensions confirmed (with Russell Green quotes)
- No evidence of continuation beyond 2020-2021

**Impact on Counter-Case**:
**MIXED - Partially supports, partially weakens**. Deutsche Bank remains actively engaged with FINOS (Fluxnova is significant), but has clearly NOT continued CDM-specific work. The explicit mention of other G-SIBs in CDM/DRR production contexts, with Deutsche Bank absent, is meaningful negative evidence. Fluxnova is process orchestration, NOT data modeling/CDM.

- General technology careers messaging ("strengthening internal technology expertise")
- **NO job postings explicitly mentioning CDM or Common Domain Model**

**Additional search**: `site:linkedin.com "Deutsche Bank" CDM engineer architect`
- No LinkedIn profiles or posts found discussing CDM work at Deutsche Bank

**Impact on Counter-Case**:
**WEAKENS counter-case significantly**. If Deutsche Bank were actively implementing CDM, we would expect:
- Job postings mentioning CDM, DRR, or ISDA digital standards
- LinkedIn posts from employees about CDM-related work
- CDM-specific technical roles

The complete absence of these signals suggests no active CDM implementation effort. Comparison: JPMorgan, Barclays, and Goldman Sachs have visible CDM-related hiring and employee activity.

## Search 4 (Additional): FINOS Governance and Current Activity

**Results Found**:

**Impact on Counter-Case**:

## Search 5 (Additional): Regulatory Reporting Context

**Query**: `"Deutsche Bank" "regulatory reporting" EMIR CFTC rewrite derivatives 2024 2025`

**Results Found**:
- Extensive industry information about EMIR Refit (April 2024 EU, September 2024 UK) and CFTC Rewrite Phase 2 (January 2024)
- No specific mentions of Deutsche Bank's approach to these regulatory changes
- Other banks and vendors prominently mentioned in compliance contexts
- BaFin fined Deutsche Bank EUR 23.05 million in February 2025 "for taking too long to investigate and correct issues in areas like derivatives trading"

**Impact on Counter-Case**:
**WEAKENS counter-case**. The BaFin fine suggests Deutsche Bank may be struggling with derivatives infrastructure rather than leading with CDM adoption. A bank actively implementing CDM/DRR for regulatory reporting would likely highlight this capability. The absence of Deutsche Bank from regulatory reporting modernization coverage is notable.

## Combined Impact Assessment

### Evidence Summary

| Search | Finding | Impact on ARCHITECT Case |
|--------|---------|-------------------------|
| CDM Technology Roadmap | No CDM in any technology announcements | WEAKENS |
| FINOS CDM/DRR Activity | Active in Fluxnova, ABSENT from CDM/DRR | MIXED (net weak) |
| Employee/Hiring Signals | Zero CDM-related signals | WEAKENS |
| FINOS Governance | No longer board leadership | SLIGHTLY WEAKENS |
| Regulatory Reporting | Absent from coverage, recent BaFin fine | WEAKENS |

### Overall Assessment

**The disconfirming searches FAILED to find evidence that would overturn the PRAGMATIST classification.**

The searches were specifically designed to find positive evidence of ARCHITECT behavior. Instead, they revealed:

1. **Active FINOS participation, but in non-CDM areas** (Fluxnova = process orchestration)
2. **Explicit absence** from CDM/DRR production activity where peer banks are named
3. **Zero hiring or employee signals** related to CDM
4. **Regulatory challenges** (BaFin fine) rather than leadership

The one positive finding (Fluxnova participation) does not indicate CDM adoption. It indicates continued commitment to open source collaboration, but in a different domain (process automation vs. data standardization).

**Conclusion**: The disconfirming searches strengthen rather than weaken the PRAGMATIST classification. Deutsche Bank appears to have pivoted from CDM-focused work (2020-2021) to other open source priorities (Waltz, Fluxnova) while peer banks have advanced with CDM production deployment.
