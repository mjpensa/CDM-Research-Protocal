## Evidence Blocks

### Evidence Block 1

**Source**: FINOS Press Release - Goldman Sachs Open Sources its Data Modeling Platform through FINOS
**URL**: https://www.finos.org/press/goldman-sachs-open-sources-its-data-modeling-platform-through-finos
**Date**: October 19, 2020
**Source Type**: FINOS official
**Tier**: 1

**Finding**:
Deutsche Bank participated in a six-month FINOS Legend pilot alongside Goldman Sachs, Morgan Stanley, RBC Capital Markets, Itau Unibanco, ScottLogic, Wells Fargo, Digital Asset, ISDA, and REGnosys. The pilot used "a shared version of Legend hosted on FINOS infrastructure in the public cloud to prototype interbank collaborative data modeling and standardization, in particular to build extensions to the Common Domain Model (CDM) developed by ISDA." The pilot group focused on "FX option extensions to the CDM, as well as nascent work on commodities reference data."

**Interpretation**:
Deutsche Bank actively contributed to CDM development through the FINOS Legend pilot, demonstrating hands-on technical engagement with CDM modeling standards. This positions them as an early collaborator in the open-source CDM ecosystem.

**Strength**: Strong
**Direction**: ARCHITECT

### Evidence Block 2

**Source Type**: FINOS official

**Finding**:

**Interpretation**:
Senior Deutsche Bank technology leadership expressed explicit enthusiasm for CDM-related tooling, signaling strategic interest in collaborative data modeling standards. Russell Green's position as FINOS board member indicates institutional commitment to open-source financial technology standards.

### Evidence Block 3

**Source**: FINOS Legend Case Study 2021
**URL**: https://www.finos.org/hubfs/FINOS/assets/FINOS%20Legend%20Case%20Study%202021.pdf
**Date**: 2021
**Source Type**: FINOS official
**Tier**: 1

**Finding**:
The FX option extensions to the CDM, specifically the "Averaging Model used in the CDM, modeled collaboratively by the pilot group financial institutions participants using Legend, were proposed into the CDM and have since been accepted, released and integrated into a recent release." Deutsche Bank was explicitly named as one of the pilot participants who contributed to this work.

**Interpretation**:
Deutsche Bank's CDM contributions were not merely exploratory - they resulted in actual production-integrated CDM extensions. This demonstrates successful translation of pilot work into accepted CDM standards, indicating technical depth and follow-through.

**Strength**: Strong
**Direction**: ARCHITECT

## Summary

**Total Evidence Blocks**: 7
**Strength Distribution**: 4 Strong, 3 Moderate, 0 Weak
**Direction Distribution**: 5 ARCHITECT signals, 1 PRAGMATIST signals, 1 NEUTRAL

**Key Takeaway**: Tier 1 evidence reveals Deutsche Bank as a significant CDM ecosystem participant through their FINOS Legend pilot work (2020-2021), where they contributed FX option extensions that were accepted into production CDM releases. Their FINOS board leadership positions and ongoing open-source contributions demonstrate institutional commitment to collaborative financial technology standards. However, direct evidence of current (2024-2025) CDM production deployment or "production expected 2025" claims was NOT found in Tier 1 official sources. The strongest evidence dates from 2020-2021 pilot participation.
