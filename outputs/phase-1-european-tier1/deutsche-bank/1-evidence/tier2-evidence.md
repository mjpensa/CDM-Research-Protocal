## Evidence Blocks

### Evidence Block 1

**Source**: Linux Foundation Press Release - Goldman Sachs Open Sources its Data Modeling Platform through FINOS
**URL**: https://www.linuxfoundation.org/press/press-release/goldman-sachs-open-sources-its-data-modeling-platform-through-finos
**Date**: October 19, 2020
**Source Type**: Industry publication / Linux Foundation
**Tier**: 2

**Finding**:
Deutsche Bank participated in a six-month pilot with Goldman Sachs, Morgan Stanley, RBC Capital Markets, and others using FINOS Legend to build extensions to the Common Domain Model (CDM). Russell Green, Head of Group Architecture at Deutsche Bank and FINOS board member, stated: "From our participation in the FINOS pilot, we believe that Legend Studio holds promise to enhance collaborative and federated data architecture and modeling within the bank and the industry."

**Interpretation**:
Confirms Deutsche Bank's active CDM pilot participation in 2020, but this is historical evidence, not current. The quote expresses belief in "promise" and intention to "explore," not production deployment.

**Strength**: Strong
**Direction**: ARCHITECT
**Temporal Relevance**: Historical (2020)
