## Regional Context

Chinese banks operate in regulatory ecosystem governed by:
- **PBOC** (People's Bank of China): Monetary policy, FX controls
- **CBIRC** (China Banking and Insurance Regulatory Commission): Banking supervision
- **CSRC** (China Securities Regulatory Commission): Derivatives markets
- **SAFE** (State Administration of Foreign Exchange): FX derivatives

None of these regulators have signaled CDM adoption requirements. China's derivatives markets use domestic standards (CFETS for FX, exchanges for commodities/equity derivatives).
