## Summary

No Tier 1 evidence found for ICBC's CDM adoption.

## Sources Checked

- **icbc.com.cn** / **icbc-ltd.com**: No CDM or ISDA Common Domain Model mentions
- **isda.org**: No ICBC case studies or working group participation
- **finos.org**: ICBC not listed as member
- **github.com/finos**: No code contributions from ICBC

## Regulatory Context

ICBC operates under Chinese regulatory framework (CBIRC, PBOC) which does not mandate CDM for derivatives reporting. China uses domestic standards (CFETS for FX, SHFE for commodities) rather than ISDA CDM.

## Analysis

The absence of Tier 1 evidence is expected given:
1. Chinese regulatory independence from Western standards
2. No PBOC/CBIRC mandate for CDM adoption
3. ICBC focus on domestic markets and Belt & Road financing
4. Limited Western disclosure by Chinese state-owned banks
