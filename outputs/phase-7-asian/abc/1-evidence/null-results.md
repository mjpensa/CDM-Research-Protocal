1. **Regulatory Context**: China's derivatives markets use domestic standards (CFETS, exchanges), not ISDA CDM
2. **No Regulatory Driver**: PBOC/CBIRC have not mandated CDM for reporting
3. **Market Structure**: Chinese banks' derivatives operations focus on domestic counterparties and onshore markets
4. **Disclosure Practices**: Chinese state-owned banks have limited Western-facing technology disclosure
5. **Strategic Priority**: Banks focus on retail digital transformation, Belt & Road financing, not wholesale derivatives infrastructure

### Regional Pattern

All four Chinese "Big Four" banks (ICBC, Bank of China, CCB, ABC) show identical null results, suggesting systemic non-adoption rather than search failure.
