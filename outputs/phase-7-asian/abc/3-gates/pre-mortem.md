# Pre-Mortem Analysis

**Research Date**: 2025-12-21

---

## Exercise Purpose

Identify failure modes before research begins.

**Scenario**: "Our assessment was completely wrong. What went wrong?"

---

## Failure Scenario 1: Missed Chinese-Language Evidence

### How We Failed
Bank has internal CDM initiatives documented only in Chinese, which our search missed.

### Mitigation
- Search Chinese financial databases (CNKI, Wanfang)
- Use CDM Chinese translations: 通用领域模型, ISDA数据标准化
- Check PBOC/CBIRC regulatory consultation documents

---

## Failure Scenario 2: International Subsidiary Adoption

### How We Failed
Bank's London/New York/Hong Kong subsidiary adopted CDM independently, not disclosed at group level.

### Mitigation
- Search for subsidiary-specific announcements
- Check UK FCA/US CFTC filings for international branches
- Review ISDA working group membership lists for subsidiary staff

---

## Failure Scenario 3: Vendor Implementation Under NDA

### How We Failed
Bank using vendor CDM solution (Bloomberg, Refinitiv) under strict confidentiality.

### Mitigation
- Check vendor case studies and whitepapers
- Search for vendor press releases (even if bank doesn't confirm)
- Look for technology partnership announcements

---

## Success Criteria

Research succeeds if we:
1. Determine with high confidence whether CDM evidence exists
2. Understand Chinese regulatory context for derivatives reporting
3. Compare findings across all Big Four banks for pattern validation
