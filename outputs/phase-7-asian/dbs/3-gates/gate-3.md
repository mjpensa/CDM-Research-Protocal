## Confidence Justification

### Why 55% (not higher)?

1. **Narrow Evidence Base**: Only 2 items, both about one individual
2. **No Corroboration**: No independent verification of organizational CDM strategy
3. **Individual vs. Institutional Ambiguity**: Unclear if Andrew Ng's roles reflect DBS strategy or personal expertise
4. **Trust Flags**: LOW_TIER_ONLY, MISSING_CORROBORATION, SINGLE_INDIVIDUAL_SIGNAL

### Why 55% (not lower)?

1. **Verified Sources**: ISDA.org and MAS.gov.sg are high-authority Tier 2 sources
2. **Current Evidence**: Both items < 12 months old (weight: 1.0)
3. **Rare Signal**: ISDA Board membership is rare and significant
4. **No Contradictions**: Zero disconfirming evidence found
