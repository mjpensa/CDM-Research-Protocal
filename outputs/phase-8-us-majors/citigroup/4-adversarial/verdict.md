## Verdict

**Classification**: OBSERVER (Ecosystem-Engaged)
**Confidence**: 50%
**Alternative Classification**: PRAGMATIST (Vendor-Dependent) at 35%

### Reasoning

The adversarial review identified legitimate weaknesses in the evidence base and plausible alternative explanations. However, the protocol requires evidence-based classification, not speculation.

**Why OBSERVER Classification Stands**:
1. Zero Tier 1 evidence despite exhaustive search of official sources
2. Single Tier 2 evidence item (FINOS membership) is not CDM-specific
3. FINOS engagement shows awareness but not technical adoption
4. Vendor proxy hypothesis is plausible but unverified

**Why PRAGMATIST Alternative Has Merit**:
1. G16 dealer profile creates strong incentive for CDM
2. Known vendor platform usage (Murex) includes CDM capabilities
3. Regulatory deadlines (CFTC 2025) favor adoption
4. Quiet implementation via vendors is common industry pattern

**Confidence Adjustment**: Maintain 50% confidence for OBSERVER, increase PRAGMATIST alternative to 35% (from initial lower probability)
