## Overview

## Steelman Thesis

**Claim**: Citigroup should be classified as PRAGMATIST (Vendor-Dependent) with moderate confidence.

**Core Argument**: Given Citigroup profile as major G16 dealer with regulatory obligations, industry-standard vendor platforms, and recent FINOS engagement, the most probable scenario is quiet CDM adoption through vendor channels.

---

## Best Evidence for Alternative Classification

### Evidence 1: G16 Dealer Profile
- Major derivatives dealer across all asset classes
- Subject to CFTC Rewrite, EMIR Refit, Basel III reporting
- Processes high volumes where standardization benefits are greatest

### Evidence 2: Vendor Platform Usage
- Known Murex user for derivatives trading
- Murex MX.3 includes ISDA CDM integration
- Industry pattern: Vendors embed CDM, banks benefit passively

### Evidence 3: FINOS Membership (Oct-Nov 2024)
- Active engagement (hackathon host)
- Timing suggests strategic interest
- First India hackathon demonstrates investment

### Evidence 4: Regulatory Pressure
- CFTC Rewrite compliance deadline (2025)
- EMIR Refit UPI requirements
- Cost-benefit favors standardization

## Final Steelman Position

**Recommended Classification**: PRAGMATIST (Vendor-Dependent)
**Confidence**: 45%

**Rationale**: Major G16 dealer with vendor platforms containing CDM capabilities. Recent FINOS membership signals interest. Absence of announcements consistent with vendor-led implementation.

**Alternative**: OBSERVER (Ecosystem-Engaged) at 40% remains plausible given evidence gaps.
