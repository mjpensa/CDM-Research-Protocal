## Executive Summary

Citigroup Inc. is classified as an **OBSERVER (Ecosystem-Engaged)** with 50% confidence. As a G16 derivatives dealer and global systemically important bank (G-SIB), Citigroup demonstrates active engagement with the FINOS ecosystem through membership and hosting the first FINOS-supported hackathon in India (October-November 2024). However, there is no evidence of direct CDM contribution, production usage, or pilot initiatives. This positions Citigroup as ecosystem-aware but not actively pursuing CDM adoption, distinguishing it from PRAGMATIST peers like JPMorgan and Goldman Sachs.

**Key Finding**: Citigroup's FINOS engagement (hackathon hosting) signals organizational interest in open-source financial infrastructure, but absence of CDM-specific activity suggests CDM is not a strategic priority despite the bank's massive derivatives operations.
