# Steelman Analysis: Bank of America Corporation

**Research Phase**: 8 (US Major Banks)
**Bank**: Bank of America Corporation
**Date**: 2025-12-21

---

## Steelman Thesis

**Claim**: Classification should be OBSERVER (Traditional) rather than PRAGMATIST (Traditional).

**Core Argument**: Evidence base is too weak for PRAGMATIST classification. Single generic ISDA protocol evidence item does not differentiate BofA from any derivatives dealer.

---

## Best Counter-Evidence

1. No FINOS membership (unlike Citigroup)
2. No vendor announcements (unlike typical PRAGMATIST pattern)
3. No regulatory compliance technology announcements
4. Single evidence item is non-differentiating

---

## Alternative Classification

**OBSERVER (Traditional)**: 40% confidence
**PRAGMATIST (Traditional)**: 35% confidence
**Unknown**: 25% confidence

**Rationale**: Insufficient evidence to confidently place in PRAGMATIST category. Evidence absence is more consistent with OBSERVER.

---

**Purpose**: Strongest counter-argument to test classification
