## Verdict Rationale

### Classification: PRAGMATIST (Regulatory-Driven)

HSBC exhibits the pattern of a **Regulatory-Driven PRAGMATIST**:

1. **Compliance-Focused Technology**: Using traditional DTCC Trade Repository infrastructure for EMIR Refit compliance (documented on official EMIR REFIT page as of April 29, 2024)
2. **DRR Pilot as Exploration**: Participated in 2018-2019 UK FCA/BoE DRR Pilot but did not graduate to production; pilot concluded that "appropriate solution not yet identified"
3. **Traditional Platforms**: Calypso adoption (2010) for derivatives technology shows reliance on established vendor platforms, not open architecture approaches
4. **No Ongoing CDM Engagement**: Zero FINOS CDM contributions despite global scale and regulatory reporting sophistication
5. **No Hiring Signals**: No CDM-specific recruitment despite managing €22.2T in derivatives
6. **No Production or Pilot Evidence**: DRR pilot was regulatory exploration (2018-2019), not production deployment or current pilot

### Sub-Classification: Regulatory-Driven

HSBC is responding to regulatory requirements (EMIR Refit April 2024, FCA reporting pressures) through traditional means rather than CDM innovation. This places them in the "Regulatory-Driven" variant of PRAGMATIST:
- Meeting compliance deadlines through established vendors (DTCC)
- Adapting existing platforms (Calypso) rather than implementing new architecture
- Not investing in CDM transformation despite regulatory incentives
