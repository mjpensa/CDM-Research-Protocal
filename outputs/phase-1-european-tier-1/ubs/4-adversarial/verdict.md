1. **Historical Interest (No Current Action)**: 2020 DAML pilot with Vinay Srinivas shows past engagement, but 5+ years of silence indicates deprioritization
2. **Acquired Capability (Unclear Continuation)**: Credit Suisse CDM team and expertise were acquired in March 2023 merger, but no evidence of post-acquisition CDM work

5. **Strong Derivatives Performance Without CDM**: Risk.net 2024 award recognizes "strongest platform we have ever had" with ZERO CDM mention despite covering current technology

UBS differs from other PRAGMATIST variants:

- **NOT Vendor-Dependent**: No evidence of outsourced CDM platform use

This sub-classification reflects the **time-bound nature** of the constraint:
- Constraint is real and binding through 2026
- Constraint will lift post-integration
- Strategic direction post-2026 is undecided
