## Executive Summary

UBS Group AG is classified as a **PRAGMATIST (Integration-Constrained)** with 60% confidence. UBS has **demonstrated CDM capability** through the 2020 ISDA/Digital Asset DAML pilot, but the Credit Suisse integration ("largest data migration in financial services history") has consumed all technology capacity. The integration-constraint hypothesis is **CONFIRMED**.

## 5. Unique Pattern: Historical Engagement + Current Constraint

Unlike Deutsche Bank and Société Générale (who show no CDM engagement), UBS has:
- **Demonstrated capability**: 2020 pilot with senior endorsement (Vinay Srinivas)
- **Acquired capability**: Credit Suisse CDM expertise (Sunil Challa)
- **Current constraint**: All capacity consumed by integration

This creates a **different classification** than pure non-engagement. UBS is PRAGMATIST due to **temporary constraint**, not lack of interest.
