## Verdict: CLASSIFICATION UPHELD

---

## Reasoning

### 1. Evidence Exists (Not UNKNOWN)

The fundamental distinction between UniCredit and ING (UNKNOWN) is the presence of verified evidence:

| Bank | ISDA Evidence | FINOS Evidence | Classification |
|------|--------------|---------------|---------------|
| ING | None | None | UNKNOWN (30%) |
| UniCredit | Historical (2011-2016) | None | OBSERVER (40%) |

Having historical evidence, even if dated, is qualitatively different from having no evidence. OBSERVER (Historical-Engagement) captures this difference.

**Counter-case weakness:** Treating UniCredit and ING identically ignores verified ISDA Board membership.

---

### 2. Sub-Classification Resolves Temporal Issue

The "Historical-Engagement" sub-classification explicitly addresses the counter-case concern about evidence age:

- **Not claiming:** Current CDM ecosystem observation
- **Claiming:** Past ISDA ecosystem engagement that predates/ended before CDM

This is a factually accurate statement that conveys useful information.

**Counter-case weakness:** Doesn't acknowledge that sub-classification system solves the temporal problem.

---

### 3. Protocol Compliance: "Not Alone" Requirement

CLAUDE.md Section 6 states historical evidence "cannot drive classification ALONE."

**Classification Drivers:**
1. Historical Tier 1 evidence (TJ Lim board membership)
2. Tier 2 null results (4 searches, no evidence)
3. Tier 3 null results (3 searches, no evidence)
4. Bayesian posterior (51.0% for OBSERVER)
5. Informative absence pattern (engagement ended)

The classification rests on five pillars, not historical evidence alone. Protocol requirement satisfied.

**Counter-case weakness:** Interprets "alone" too narrowly; doesn't account for null results as evidence.

---

### 4. Null Results Corroborate Historical Narrative

The 10:1 null-to-positive ratio doesn't contradict OBSERVER (Historical-Engagement); it defines it:

**What 10:1 Ratio Tells Us:**
- Past engagement (1 positive evidence item)
- No current engagement (10 null results)
- Pattern: engagement ended

This pattern matches OBSERVER (Historical-Engagement) perfectly.

**Counter-case weakness:** Views null results as contradiction rather than corroboration.

---

### 5. Confidence Calibration Reflects Uncertainty

40% confidence appropriately captures evidence limitations:

- Lower than Credit Agricole (55%, current evidence)
- Higher than ING (30%, no evidence)
- Signals to readers: "Low-moderate confidence, evidence is limited and dated"

Reducing confidence to 30% (UNKNOWN level) would eliminate the distinction between historical evidence and no evidence.

**Counter-case weakness:** Doesn't provide alternative confidence calibration rationale.

---

### 6. Information Value to Readers

**OBSERVER (Historical-Engagement) conveys:**
- UniCredit had ISDA Board representation 2011-2016
- This engagement ended or hasn't continued into CDM era
- Bank has historical ecosystem awareness but no current CDM adoption

**UNKNOWN would convey:**
- We don't know anything about UniCredit's CDM posture
- [Loses all information about historical ISDA engagement]

The current classification is more informative and useful.

---

## Addressing Counter-Case's Strongest Point

### "Evidence >3 years cannot drive classification alone"

**Counter-case interpretation:** Historical evidence should not produce any classification above UNKNOWN.

**Steelman interpretation:** Historical evidence, when combined with comprehensive null results showing the pattern of ended engagement, can support OBSERVER (Historical-Engagement).

**Verdict:** Steelman interpretation is more consistent with protocol intent.

**Rationale:**
- Protocol aims to prevent over-reliance on old evidence
- It doesn't prohibit using historical evidence as one input among many
- The 0.3 weight multiplier and 40% confidence cap enforce temporal discount
- Sub-classification clearly signals historical nature

**The spirit of the rule:** Don't make confident claims based solely on old evidence.
**Compliance:** We're making a low-confidence (40%) claim based on historical evidence + null results.

---

## Alternative Perspective: Why Rejecting Counter-Case

If we accepted the counter-case logic (historical evidence → UNKNOWN), we'd have to:

1. **Ignore verified Tier 1 evidence** (ISDA Board membership)
2. **Treat "no evidence" and "historical evidence" identically** (both → UNKNOWN)
3. **Lose informational value** (readers don't learn about historical engagement)
4. **Create arbitrary threshold** (how old is too old for any classification?)
5. **Invalidate sub-classification system** (Historical-Engagement becomes meaningless)

These costs outweigh the benefits of stricter temporal interpretation.
