## Classification Rationale

### Why OBSERVER (51.0%)

1. **Historical ISDA Board membership** (TJ Lim 2011-2016) is classic observer signal
2. **Comprehensive null results** across all tiers (10 null results)
3. **No continuity** of engagement since 2016
4. **Informative absence** strongly suggests limited/ended engagement
5. **Bayesian posterior** favors OBSERVER over PRAGMATIST

### Why Not PRAGMATIST (47.9%)

1. **No vendor signals** found in trade press
2. **No platform announcements** involving UniCredit
3. Historical board membership suggests at least awareness of ISDA ecosystem
4. Pattern doesn't match typical PRAGMATIST (who have vendor relationships)

### Why Historical-Engagement Sub-Classification

1. Evidence is 9 years old (ended 2016)
2. No current ISDA/CDM engagement
3. Predates CDM development (2017)
4. No evidence of transition from historical to current engagement
