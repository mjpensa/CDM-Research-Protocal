# Counter-Case Analysis: ING Group

**Bank:** ING Group
**Phase:** 4 - European Tier 2
**Analysis Date:** 2025-12-20

---

## Purpose

Devil's advocate analysis challenging the proposed UNKNOWN classification. This document presents the strongest case against the current classification.

## Proposed Classification

| Classification | Sub-Classification | Confidence |
|----------------|-------------------|------------|
| UNKNOWN | Insufficient-Evidence | 30% |

## Counter-Case: ING Should Be Classified PRAGMATIST

### Argument 1: Absence of Evidence IS Evidence

**Claim:** A major European bank handling billions in derivatives cannot be "unknown" - they must be doing something.

**Counter-Evidence:**
- ING is a major European derivatives dealer
- EMIR Refit compliance was mandatory (April 2024)
- ING must have a reporting solution in place
- If not CDM, then by definition they're using traditional approaches

**Impact:** This suggests PRAGMATIST (Traditional) rather than UNKNOWN

### Argument 2: Implicit Vendor Usage

**Claim:** Large banks typically use established vendors for regulatory reporting.

**Counter-Evidence:**
- DTCC is the dominant European trade repository
- Major banks rarely build in-house reporting solutions
- ING's silence on CDM likely means vendor reliance
- Murex, Calypso, or similar platforms probable

**Impact:** Supports PRAGMATIST (Vendor-Dependent) classification

### Argument 3: European Bank Patterns

**Claim:** European Tier 2 banks follow similar patterns - mostly pragmatist approaches.

**Counter-Evidence:**
- Société Générale: PRAGMATIST
- Commerzbank: PRAGMATIST (Murex)
- UniCredit: OBSERVER (historical only)
- Pattern suggests ING fits PRAGMATIST profile

**Impact:** Peer comparison supports PRAGMATIST

## Counter-Case: ING Should Be Classified OBSERVER

### Argument 4: Hidden ISDA Engagement

**Claim:** ING may participate in ISDA working groups without public disclosure.

**Counter-Evidence:**
- ISDA has hundreds of member firms
- Not all participants are publicly named
- ING's derivatives business would benefit from ISDA engagement
- Absence of public evidence ≠ absence of engagement

**Impact:** Could support OBSERVER classification

## Counter-Case: ING Should Be Classified ARCHITECT

### Argument 5: Confidential CDM Adoption

**Claim:** ING may be implementing CDM without announcement.

**Counter-Evidence:**
- Some firms adopt before announcing
- Competitive advantage in early adoption
- ING's tech-forward reputation (ING Labs, APIs)
- Possible stealth CDM project

**Impact:** Low probability but non-zero. Residual 5% ARCHITECT probability appropriate.

## Evaluation of Counter-Arguments

| Argument | Strength | Probability Shift |
|----------|----------|-------------------|
| Implicit vendor usage | Strong | +15% toward PRAGMATIST |
| European bank patterns | Medium | +10% toward PRAGMATIST |
| Hidden ISDA engagement | Weak | +5% toward OBSERVER |
| Confidential CDM adoption | Very Weak | No change |

## Revised Classification Consideration

Original: UNKNOWN (30%)
Counter-case suggests: PRAGMATIST may be more appropriate

However, without positive evidence of traditional platform usage, UNKNOWN remains defensible.

## Recommendation

Maintain **UNKNOWN (Insufficient-Evidence)** but acknowledge:
1. PRAGMATIST is a plausible alternative
2. The bank is almost certainly not an ARCHITECT
3. Further investigation would require direct inquiry
