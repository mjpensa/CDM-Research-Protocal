## Key Decision Points

### 1. Should we infer from absence?

**Decision:** Limited inference only.

- Absence of CDM evidence → ING is not publicly engaged with CDM ✓
- Absence of CDM evidence → ING uses traditional platforms ✗ (inference too far)

### 2. Is peer pattern sufficient for classification?

**Decision:** No.

- Similar banks (Commerzbank, SocGen) are PRAGMATIST
- But ING-specific evidence required for ING classification
- Pattern matching ≠ evidence

### 3. Should confidence be higher or lower?

**Decision:** 30% is appropriate.

- Could argue for 25% (more conservative)
- Could argue for 35% (ceiling for Tier 4 inference)
- 30% balances uncertainty with pattern recognition
