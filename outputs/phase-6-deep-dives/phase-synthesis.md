## Key Insights from Phase 6

### Insight 1: CDM Adoption is Strategically Diverse

Four Architects show four distinct motivations:
1. **Barclays**: UK advocacy and FMI-first strategy
2. **UBS**: Post-merger positioning
3. **Standard Chartered**: Asian market strategy
4. **Pictet**: Client service differentiation

No single "Architect profile" - adoption is driven by business-specific strategic logic.

### Insight 2: Production Remains Rare

Only 1 of 19 assessed banks (Pictet) is in confirmed production. Adding anchor points:
- BNP Paribas (not assessed but known)
- JPMorgan (not assessed but known)
- JSCC (CCP, not bank)

Production remains exceptional even among Architects. Most Architects are Followers (contributors without production).

### Insight 3: Investment Banking Not Required

Pictet demonstrates that investment banking focus is not required for CDM architecture. Private banking with sophisticated clients can drive CDM investment.

### Insight 4: Non-EU Motivation

Both Phase 6 banks have non-EU strategic motivation:
- Standard Chartered: Asian market positioning
- Pictet: Swiss/client-service (not EU regulatory)

CDM adoption extends beyond EU EMIR-driven compliance.
