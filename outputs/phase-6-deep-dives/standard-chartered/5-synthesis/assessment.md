## Strategic Analysis

### Why ARCHITECT Despite Smaller Derivatives Footprint?

Standard Chartered's CDM engagement appears driven by:

1. **Regulatory Efficiency**: Multi-jurisdictional operations (Asia, Africa, Middle East) create complex reporting requirements
2. **First-Mover Positioning**: Early contribution establishes expertise before mandatory adoption
3. **Client Services**: Emerging markets clients may value CDM-enabled reporting
4. **ISDA Governance Role**: Board membership incentivizes active contribution
