2. **German Market Position**: Major German bank after Deutsche Bank
3. **Regulatory Pressure**: EMIR Refit may drive CDM adoption
4. **mBank Subsidiary**: Polish fintech subsidiary could innovate

### Arguments AGAINST ARCHITECT
1. **No FINOS Membership**: Despite industry growth
2. **No ISDA Board**: No governance engagement
3. **2018 Enforcement**: $12M fine suggests compliance challenges
4. **No CDM Signals**: Zero evidence across all tiers
5. **Deutsche Bank Contrast**: Same market, vastly different CDM engagement

### Verdict on ARCHITECT Counter-Case
**REJECTED** - 2018 enforcement and lack of governance engagement strongly indicate traditional approach. Deutsche Bank comparison is instructive.
