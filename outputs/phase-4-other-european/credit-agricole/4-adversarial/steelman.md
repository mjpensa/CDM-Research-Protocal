# Credit Agricole - Steelman Analysis

## Strongest Case for PRAGMATIST (ISDA Governance)

**Credit Agricole has an ISDA Board member (Christine Cremel) who has served since July 2020 and been re-elected three times (2020, 2022, 2024). She leads EMIR compliance for Credit Agricole CIB. However, despite 5 years of ISDA governance participation, Credit Agricole has NOT joined FINOS and has NO CDM contribution record. This matches the "ISDA Governance" pattern seen in Japanese mega-banks (Nomura, MUFG, Mizuho) where Board participation does not translate to CDM adoption.**

**Plausibility**: 72%

## Why This Is the Strongest Case

1. **Direct Board Evidence**: Christine Cremel is verifiable on ISDA Board
2. **Long Tenure**: 5 years of Board service shows sustained engagement
3. **Clear Pattern Match**: Identical pattern to 4+ other major banks
4. **Negative Evidence Strong**: Verified NOT FINOS member via official list
5. **No Contradicting Evidence**: Zero CDM signals across all tiers
