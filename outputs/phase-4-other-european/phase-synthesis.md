## Key Insights from Phase 4

### Insight 1: PRAGMATIST is the European Default

13 of 15 banks assessed so far are PRAGMATIST. Only 2 Architects exist (Barclays, UBS), both from Phase 1 European Tier 1. CDM architecture is the exception, not the rule.

### Insight 2: National Banking Sectors Follow Leaders

Within national banking sectors:
- If the leader is Architect (BNP in France), others may be aware but don't follow
- If the leader is Pragmatist (Deutsche Bank in Germany), all are Pragmatist
- No "second mover" CDM adoption observed in any national market

### Insight 3: EMIR Refit is Not a CDM Forcing Function

All 11 European banks faced EMIR Refit (April 2024). Zero adopted CDM in response. Regulatory compliance and CDM adoption are independent decisions.

### Insight 4: Investment Banking Presence is Necessary but Not Sufficient

All Phase 4 banks have investment banking units. None are CDM Architects. IB presence creates capability but not leadership. Something additional is required:
- Strategic commitment (Barclays advocacy)
- Production deployment (BNP, UBS)
- Working group leadership (confirmed contributors)
