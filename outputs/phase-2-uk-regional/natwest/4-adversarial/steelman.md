# Steelman Argument: NatWest as ARCHITECT

**Bank**: NatWest Group plc
**Date**: 2025-12-20

---

## The Strongest Case for ARCHITECT Classification

After examining all counter-arguments, the **strongest possible case** for classifying NatWest as ARCHITECT would require the following scenario:

---

## Steelman Scenario: Silent Internal CDM Build (2020-2025)

### Premise

NatWest participated in the FCA/BoE DRR pilot (2018-2019), identified gaps, and spent 2020-2025 building internal CDM capability silently. The bank deliberately avoids public announcements until production readiness, explaining the absence of evidence.

### Supporting Logic

1. **Pilot Learnings**: The 2019 pilot identified technical gaps. NatWest may have spent 2020-2025 addressing these internally.

2. **Strategic Silence**: Unlike Fluxnova (open-source infrastructure), CDM implementation may be considered competitive advantage worth keeping confidential.

3. **FINOS Capability**: James McLeod's leadership of open-source programs demonstrates NatWest has the organizational capability to execute complex technical transformations.

4. **UK Regulatory Alignment**: As a UK-headquartered bank, NatWest has strategic incentive to align with FCA/BoE DRR vision.

5. **Derivatives Business**: While primarily retail-focused, NatWest Markets maintains derivatives operations that could benefit from CDM.

---

## Critical Evaluation of Steelman

### Why This Scenario Is Unlikely

**1. Contradicts Observed Publicity Behavior**
- NatWest publicly announces Fluxnova (October 2025)
- If NatWest were willing to publicize open-source work, why not CDM?
- "Competitive advantage" argument doesn't explain why Fluxnova is public but CDM is secret

**2. No Tier 3 Leakage**
- Silent implementations still generate hiring signals (CDM keywords in job postings)
- Employees typically list major projects on LinkedIn
- GitHub activity (even in private repos) often shows in profiles
- Complete absence of ALL Tier 3 signals suggests no implementation, not silent implementation

**3. FINOS Paradox Remains Unexplained**
- If NatWest has CDM capability, why not contribute to finos/common-domain-model?
- Contributing to open-source CDM would accelerate internal implementation
- Deliberate avoidance of FINOS CDM contribution suggests strategic choice NOT to pursue CDM

**4. 5-Year Gap Implausibility**
- 2018-2019 pilot → 2025 with zero signals is implausibly long for silent build
- Most banks announce pilots/production within 2-3 years
- Regulatory deadlines (EMIR Refit April 2024) would have driven announcements if CDM was the compliance path

**5. Alternative Explanation Is Simpler**
- Occam's Razor: Pilot abandonment is simpler than silent 5-year build
- Evidence pattern fits "pilot did not lead to production" narrative
- UK Regional bank profile (retail focus) supports lower CDM priority

---

## Probability Assessment

**P(Silent Internal Build)**: < 5%

**Why So Low?**
- Requires explaining away Fluxnova publicity contradiction
- Requires assuming zero Tier 3 leakage over 5 years
- Requires ignoring FINOS Paradox
- Conflicts with UK regulatory transparency culture
- No supporting evidence, only absence of disconfirming evidence

---

## Verdict

Even the **strongest possible case** for ARCHITECT classification (silent internal build) has < 5% probability. The steelman argument cannot overcome:

1. **Temporal Decay**: 5-year gap since pilot
2. **FINOS Paradox**: Capability without CDM application
3. **Publicity Contradiction**: Fluxnova public, CDM silent
4. **Comprehensive Absence**: No Tier 2 or Tier 3 signals
5. **Simpler Alternative**: Pilot abandonment explains all evidence

**Conclusion**: The steelman fails. OBSERVER (Historical-Engagement) classification is robust.
