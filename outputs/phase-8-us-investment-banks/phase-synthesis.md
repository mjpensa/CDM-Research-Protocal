**Protocol**: Mixed (Tier A/B)

---

## Executive Summary

Phase 8 assessed the five major US investment banks for CDM adoption posture. Only **JPMorgan** (confirmed production October 2024) classified as **ARCHITECT**. The remaining four banks classified as **PRAGMATIST** despite significant derivatives businesses.

**Key Finding**: JPMorgan stands alone among US G-SIBs as CDM adopter. Goldman Sachs, Morgan Stanley, Citigroup, and Bank of America show no CDM production signals and will likely follow vendor path.

## Key Findings

### 1. JPMorgan Is US CDM Leader

JPMorgan's October 2024 CDM production announcement establishes them as:
- Only US bank in confirmed CDM production
- US market validation for CDM standard
- Peer pressure creator for other US dealers

JPMorgan follows BNP Paribas pattern: large derivatives dealer with regulatory driver achieving production.

### 2. No US Derivatives Dealer Cohort

Unlike Japanese banks (FSA cohort) or Chinese banks (CBIRC cohort), US derivatives dealers show **no cohort behavior**:
- JPMorgan: ARCHITECT (production)
- Goldman Sachs: PRAGMATIST (no signals)
- Morgan Stanley: PRAGMATIST (wealth management focus)

The US market shows JPMorgan acting alone, with Goldman and Morgan Stanley not following.

### 3. Goldman Sachs Uncertainty

Goldman Sachs classification has **lowest confidence** (65%) due to:
- Strong proprietary technology culture (could support stealth CDM)
- Secretive technology strategy
- No positive or negative CDM signals

Classification is provisional — Goldman could announce CDM production and shift to ARCHITECT.

### 4. Universal Bank Pattern Confirmed

Citigroup and Bank of America confirm the universal bank pattern seen in Europe:

| Bank | Region | Business Model | Classification |
|------|--------|----------------|----------------|
| Citigroup | USA | Universal (restructuring) | PRAGMATIST |
| Bank of America | USA | Universal (retail focus) | PRAGMATIST |
| Deutsche Bank | Germany | Universal (restructuring) | PRAGMATIST |
| HSBC | UK | Universal | PRAGMATIST |

Universal banks consistently choose vendor path for CDM.

### 5. Wealth Management Dilution Effect

Morgan Stanley demonstrates "wealth management dilution":
- E*TRADE acquisition and wealth growth
- Technology investment prioritizes retail/wealth
- Institutional securities CDM-relevant but not prioritized

This mirrors UBS (Credit Suisse integration) — competing priorities delay CDM.

US market is **less CDM-adopted** than Europe despite JPMorgan production.

---

## CFTC Rewrite Impact

CFTC regulatory modernization has not driven widespread CDM adoption:
- JPMorgan used CFTC as CDM driver
- Goldman, Morgan Stanley addressing CFTC through existing systems
- Citigroup, Bank of America addressing through vendor solutions

CFTC Rewrite creates compliance need but **not CDM mandate** — banks choosing alternative compliance paths.

## Conclusion

Phase 8 reveals that US CDM adoption is concentrated in JPMorgan alone. Despite JPMorgan's October 2024 production announcement:
- Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley show no CDM signals

The US derivatives market will likely see JPMorgan as leader with others following vendor path for CFTC compliance.
