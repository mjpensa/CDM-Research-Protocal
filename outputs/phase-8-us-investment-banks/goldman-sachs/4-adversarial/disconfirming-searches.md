# Disconfirming Evidence Searches: Goldman Sachs

**Bank**: Goldman Sachs Group, Inc.
**Phase**: 8 (US Investment Banks)
**Date**: 2025-12-21

---

## Methodology

Searches designed to find evidence that would **disprove** the PRAGMATIST (Ecosystem) classification.

---

## Disconfirming Search 1: Goldman Sachs CDM Production

**Query**: Goldman Sachs CDM production deployment live

**Hypothesis to Disprove**: Goldman Sachs is not in production with CDM

**Expected Finding if False**: Production deployment announcements

**Actual Finding**: No production deployment claims found

**Conclusion**: Hypothesis NOT disproved

---

## Disconfirming Search 2: Goldman Sachs DRR Primary

**Query**: Goldman Sachs DRR "primary mechanism" OR "primary reporting"

**Hypothesis to Disprove**: Goldman Sachs has not adopted DRR as primary mechanism

**Expected Finding if False**: Language similar to JPMorgan's claims

**Actual Finding**: No such claims found; only "investing to implement"

**Conclusion**: Hypothesis NOT disproved

---

## Disconfirming Search 3: Goldman Sachs FINOS Maintainer

**Query**: Goldman Sachs CDM maintainer FINOS

**Hypothesis to Disprove**: Goldman Sachs has no CDM maintainer status

**Expected Finding if False**: Maintainer appointment announcement

**Actual Finding**: No maintainer from Goldman Sachs; only JPMorgan (Nick Moger)

**Conclusion**: Hypothesis NOT disproved

---

## Disconfirming Search 4: Goldman Sachs Regulatory Reporting Award

**Query**: Goldman Sachs DRR award regulatory reporting FINOS

**Hypothesis to Disprove**: Goldman Sachs has not received production adoption recognition

**Expected Finding if False**: Adoption award similar to JPMorgan's

**Actual Finding**: Legend partnership award (2021) but no adoption award

**Conclusion**: Hypothesis NOT disproved

---

## Disconfirming Search 5: Legend CDM Production

**Query**: Legend platform CDM production Goldman Sachs

**Hypothesis to Disprove**: Legend is not used for production CDM at Goldman Sachs

**Expected Finding if False**: Claims of Legend-based CDM production at Goldman Sachs

**Actual Finding**: Legend used for CDM development/collaboration, not production claims

**Conclusion**: Hypothesis NOT disproved
