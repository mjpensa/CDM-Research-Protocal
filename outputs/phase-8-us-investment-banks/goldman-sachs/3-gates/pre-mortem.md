# Pre-Mortem Analysis: Goldman Sachs

**Bank**: Goldman Sachs Group, Inc.
**Phase**: 8 (US Investment Banks)
**Date**: 2025-12-21

---

## Pre-Mortem Question

"If this research fails to find accurate CDM adoption evidence for Goldman Sachs, what will have caused the failure?"

---

## Potential Failure Modes

### 1. Legend Focus Bias (MEDIUM RISK)

**Failure Mode**: Overemphasis on Legend contribution obscures production CDM usage

**Mitigation Applied**:
- Searched specifically for "Goldman Sachs CDM production" and "Goldman Sachs DRR"
- Found "investing to implement" language, not production claims
- Compared explicitly to JPMorgan's language

**Residual Risk**: Medium - production usage could exist but be less publicized

---

### 2. Internal vs External Communication (MEDIUM RISK)

**Failure Mode**: Goldman Sachs may have production CDM but not publicize it

**Mitigation Applied**:
- Searched trade press, FINOS sources, and official Goldman channels
- No production claims found across multiple source types

**Residual Risk**: Medium - Goldman Sachs may be less public than JPMorgan

---

### 3. Timeline Gap (LOW RISK)

**Failure Mode**: Goldman Sachs may have achieved production since "investing" statement

**Mitigation Applied**:
- Evidence is current (2024-2025)
- No newer production announcements found

**Residual Risk**: Low - would expect announcement if production achieved

---

### 4. Classification Boundary (MEDIUM RISK)

**Failure Mode**: PRAGMATIST vs ARCHITECT boundary may be incorrect

**Mitigation Applied**:
- Clear criteria: production usage = ARCHITECT, contribution without production = PRAGMATIST
- Goldman Sachs has exceptional contribution but no production claim

**Residual Risk**: Medium - boundary could be debated

---

## Highest Risk Factors

1. **Communication style difference** - Goldman Sachs may be less public than JPMorgan
2. **Legend overshadowing** - Contribution focus may obscure deployment status
3. **Temporal gap** - "Investing" status may have changed

---

## Pre-Mortem Conclusion

The research accurately captures Goldman Sachs' exceptional ecosystem contribution while noting the absence of production claims. The main uncertainty is whether Goldman Sachs has production usage but hasn't publicized it.
