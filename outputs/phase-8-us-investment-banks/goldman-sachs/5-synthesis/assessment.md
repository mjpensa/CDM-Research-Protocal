## Key Findings

### 1. Legend Platform Contribution

Goldman Sachs developed and open-sourced Legend to FINOS in October 2020:
- Flagship data management and governance platform
- Used by multiple investment banks for CDM collaboration
- Enabled FX option extensions to be accepted into CDM
- Won 2021 Banking Tech Award for Best Fintech Partnership

**Impact**: Major ecosystem contribution, not production deployment.

### 2. CDM Model Contribution

Goldman Sachs led a 6-month pilot with Deutsche Bank, Morgan Stanley, and RBC:
- Developed FX option extensions (Averaging Model)
- Extensions accepted and integrated into CDM
- Demonstrates technical capability and industry leadership

**Impact**: Direct contribution to CDM standard.

### 3. DRR Investment (Not Production)

REGnosys explicitly states Goldman Sachs is "investing to implement DRR":
- This language is clearly pre-production
- Contrasts with JPMorgan's "primary mechanism" language
- Confirms active work but not deployment

**Impact**: Key differentiator from ARCHITECT classification.
