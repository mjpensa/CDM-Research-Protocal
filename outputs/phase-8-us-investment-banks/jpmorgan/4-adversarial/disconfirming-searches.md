# Disconfirming Evidence Searches: JPMorgan

**Bank**: JPMorgan Chase & Co.
**Phase**: 8 (US Investment Banks)
**Date**: 2025-12-21

---

## Methodology

Searches designed to find evidence that would **disprove** the ARCHITECT (Native) classification.

---

## Disconfirming Search 1: JPMorgan CDM Failure

**Query**: JPMorgan CDM failure problem issue

**Hypothesis to Disprove**: JPMorgan CDM implementation is successful

**Expected Finding if False**: Reports of CDM implementation problems or failures

**Actual Finding**: No negative coverage found. All coverage is positive or neutral.

**Conclusion**: Hypothesis NOT disproved

---

## Disconfirming Search 2: JPMorgan CDM Pilot Only

**Query**: JPMorgan CDM pilot proof of concept POC

**Hypothesis to Disprove**: JPMorgan is in production (not pilot)

**Expected Finding if False**: Descriptions of JPMorgan CDM as pilot or POC

**Actual Finding**: All sources describe production usage, not pilot

**Conclusion**: Hypothesis NOT disproved

---

## Disconfirming Search 3: JPMorgan CDM Vendor Dependence

**Query**: JPMorgan CDM REGnosys TradeHeader vendor outsource

**Hypothesis to Disprove**: JPMorgan has native CDM capability (not vendor-dependent)

**Expected Finding if False**: Evidence that JPMorgan relies entirely on vendors

**Actual Finding**: While vendors are part of ecosystem, Nick Moger's maintainer status proves native capability

**Conclusion**: Hypothesis NOT disproved

---

## Disconfirming Search 4: JPMorgan CDM Limited Scope

**Query**: JPMorgan CDM limited scope equity only

**Hypothesis to Disprove**: JPMorgan CDM covers broad scope

**Expected Finding if False**: Evidence that CDM only covers narrow asset class

**Actual Finding**: Multi-jurisdictional coverage (ASIC, MAS, EMIR) suggests broad scope

**Conclusion**: Hypothesis NOT disproved

---

## Disconfirming Search 5: Other US Bank CDM First

**Query**: "first US bank" CDM DRR NOT JPMorgan

**Hypothesis to Disprove**: JPMorgan is the first major US bank with CDM/DRR

**Expected Finding if False**: Another US bank claiming first-mover status

**Actual Finding**: No competing claims found

**Conclusion**: Hypothesis NOT disproved
