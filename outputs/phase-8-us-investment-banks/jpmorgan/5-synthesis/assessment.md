## Key Findings

### 1. First Major US Bank with CDM/DRR as Primary Mechanism

JPMorgan explicitly claims:
> "JPMorgan Chase is proud to be the first major U.S. bank to implement CDM/DRR as a primary reporting mechanism."

This claim is:
- Made on official JPMorgan technology blog
- Verified by FINOS independent sources
- Not contested by any competitor

### 2. First Sell-Side CDM Maintainer

Nick Moger (Executive Director, Regulatory Technology Product Director) was appointed as the first sell-side CDM maintainer at FINOS. FINOS stated:
> "The addition of a maintainer from J.P. Morgan—a leading bank with the CDM in production—ensures the model's evolution reflects the practical, everyday challenges and requirements of its adopters."

This confirms:
- JPMorgan has CDM in production
- JPMorgan has internal CDM expertise at maintainer level
- JPMorgan is contributing to CDM governance

### 3. Multi-Jurisdictional DRR Deployment

JPMorgan has confirmed DRR implementation for:
- **ASIC** (Australia) - "in the middle" of implementation
- **MAS** (Singapore) - yielding positive results
- **EMIR** - participating in G-SIB project
- **CFTC** - US regulatory coverage

### 4. Industry Recognition

JPMorgan received FINOS's 'Adoption Achiever' award at the Open Source Finance Forum in New York, specifically recognizing production CDM adoption.
