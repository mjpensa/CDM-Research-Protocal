# Pre-Mortem Analysis: JPMorgan

**Bank**: JPMorgan Chase & Co.
**Phase**: 8 (US Investment Banks)
**Date**: 2025-12-21

---

## Pre-Mortem Question

"If this research fails to find accurate CDM adoption evidence for JPMorgan, what will have caused the failure?"

---

## Potential Failure Modes

### 1. Official Claim Overstated (LOW RISK)

**Failure Mode**: JPMorgan's claim of "first major US bank" overstates actual deployment

**Mitigation Applied**:
- Cross-referenced with FINOS official sources
- Verified maintainer appointment independently
- Confirmed multi-source corroboration (ISDA, FINOS, trade press)

**Residual Risk**: Low - multiple independent sources confirm production usage

---

### 2. "Primary Mechanism" Misinterpretation (LOW RISK)

**Failure Mode**: "Primary reporting mechanism" could mean less than full production

**Mitigation Applied**:
- FINOS confirms "CDM in production" independently
- Nick Moger's conference presentations confirm production status
- 'Adoption Achiever' award specifically recognizes production usage

**Residual Risk**: Low - terminology is consistent across sources

---

### 3. Scope Limitation (MEDIUM RISK)

**Failure Mode**: CDM/DRR may only cover subset of derivatives reporting

**Mitigation Applied**:
- Evidence confirms ASIC (Australia) and MAS (Singapore) regimes
- EMIR Refit participation confirmed
- Multi-jurisdictional scope established

**Residual Risk**: Medium - full asset class coverage not specified

---

### 4. Temporal Risk (LOW RISK)

**Failure Mode**: Evidence becomes stale as implementation progresses or changes

**Mitigation Applied**:
- All evidence from 2024-2025 (current)
- Maintainer status is ongoing commitment
- Production usage is established fact, not future plan

**Residual Risk**: Low - evidence is current and stable

---

### 5. Competitive Positioning (LOW RISK)

**Failure Mode**: "First US bank" claim could be marketing rather than technical fact

**Mitigation Applied**:
- Claim verified by FINOS (neutral third party)
- ISDA webinar involvement confirms substantive commitment
- Technical details (maintainer status) cannot be fabricated

**Residual Risk**: Low - independent verification available

---

## Highest Risk Factors

1. **Scope limitation** - exact asset class/jurisdiction coverage unclear
2. **Definition ambiguity** - "primary mechanism" interpretation
3. **Selection bias** - JPMorgan's public communications may highlight success

---

## Pre-Mortem Conclusion

This is one of the lowest-risk assessments in the research protocol. Multiple independent sources confirm production CDM/DRR usage. The primary residual risk is scope limitation rather than existence of adoption.
