## Classification Rationale

SMBC is classified as OBSERVER (CCP-Connected) based on:

1. **Indirect Infrastructure Connection:** As a JSCC clearing member, SMBC has mandated connectivity to CDM infrastructure deployed by JSCC in production (June 2025)
2. **No Internal Development Evidence:** Zero Tier 1 evidence of CDM capabilities
3. **No Strategic Positioning:** No hiring for CDM expertise, no vendor partnerships announced
4. **No Public Commitment:** Absence of announcements, trade press coverage, or working group participation
5. **Regulatory Compliance Mode:** CCP-Connected classification acknowledges that SMBC's CDM exposure is infrastructure-driven rather than strategic

The CCP-Connected sub-classification distinguishes SMBC from PRAGMATIST or ARCHITECT classifications by recognizing that CDM exposure is externally mandated (JSCC regulatory requirement) rather than self-directed strategic adoption.

## Confidence Assessment

- **Tier Limitation:** No Tier 1 evidence caps confidence per CLAUDE.md protocol

SMBC is an OBSERVER with infrastructure-mandated CDM exposure through JSCC clearing membership. No evidence of strategic CDM adoption, internal capability building, or vendor partnerships. The bank's CDM exposure is passive and reactive (JSCC regulatory requirement) rather than proactive and strategic.

Classification is stable and well-supported by:

- Consistent null results across all evidence tiers
