## 4. Classification Rationale

Mitsubishi UFJ Financial Group is classified as **OBSERVER (CCP-Connected)** based on:

1. **Indirect connection to JSCC CDM infrastructure** - MUFG is a clearing member of JSCC, which is the first CCP globally to deploy CDM in production (June 2025). However, this is infrastructure-level exposure, not strategic adoption.

2. **No evidence of internal CDM development** - Comprehensive searches of official sources yielded no CDM announcements or commitments.

3. **No hiring for CDM capabilities** - No job postings or recruitment signals for CDM expertise.

4. **No vendor partnerships announced** - No public disclosures of CDM vendor relationships.

5. **No trade press coverage** - No mentions in financial technology or derivatives trade publications.

The **CCP-Connected** sub-classification acknowledges infrastructure exposure while distinguishing it from strategic adoption. MUFG must interface with JSCC's CDM infrastructure for clearing, but this is a regulatory/operational requirement rather than evidence of internal CDM strategy.
