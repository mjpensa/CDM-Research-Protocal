## Cross-Bank Patterns

### Pattern 1: Japanese Cohort Behavior

All four Japanese banks exhibit remarkably similar CDM postures:

| Characteristic | Nomura | MUFG | Mizuho | SMBC |
|---------------|--------|------|--------|------|
| Classification | PRAGMATIST | PRAGMATIST | PRAGMATIST | PRAGMATIST |
| Sub-type | Vendor-Dependent | Vendor-Dependent | Vendor-Dependent | Vendor-Dependent |
| P(Architect) | 11% | 8% | 20% | 4% |
| Direct CDM Evidence | None | None | None | None |
| Vendor Pattern Expected | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |

**Insight**: Japanese banks function as a cohort on CDM adoption. The Japanese Bankers Association (JBA) coordination model and cultural preference for consensus likely drives collective, rather than competitive, standards adoption.

**Framework Contribution**: Japanese banks should be evaluated as a cohort with shared characteristics. Individual differentiation less likely than in Western markets.

---

### Pattern 2: JSCC CDM Infrastructure-Led Adoption

**Critical Context**: JSCC (Japan Securities Clearing Corporation) went live with CDM on June 27, 2025, becoming the first CCP globally to deploy CDM in production.

**Implications**:
- All major Japanese clearing members (including all Phase 3 banks) must maintain JSCC CDM connectivity
- Infrastructure requirement does NOT indicate strategic CDM commitment
- Banks can achieve connectivity via translation layers or vendor solutions
- Similar to EU EMIR Refit: compliance achievable without native CDM capability

**Observation**: JSCC CDM implementation creates baseline engagement for Japanese banks but does not differentiate Architects from Pragmatists. The key question is HOW banks connect (native vs. vendor-mediated), not WHETHER they connect.

**Framework Contribution**: CCP CDM requirements should be treated as infrastructure forcing functions, not Architect indicators. Connectivity requirement is neutral between Architect and Pragmatist hypotheses.

## Research Questions Summary

### Q1: Does JSCC CDM Indicate Japanese Bank CDM Leadership?

**Answer**: NO

JSCC CDM is infrastructure-led by the CCP, not bank-initiated. Clearing members are connectivity consumers, not CDM contributors. Pattern is analogous to EMIR Refit compliance - regulatory/infrastructure requirement achievable without strategic CDM investment.

### Q2: Is Nomura Differentiated Due to Investment Banking Focus?

**Answer**: PARTIALLY

Nomura shows higher prior (35% vs. 20-25%) and higher posterior (11% vs. 4-8% for others) but remains PRAGMATIST. Global derivatives footprint (post-Lehman) creates capability but no evidence of strategic CDM deployment.

### Q3: Will Japanese Banks Follow JSCC into CDM Adoption?

**Answer**: YES, AS PRAGMATISTS

Japanese banks will adopt CDM operationally to maintain JSCC connectivity, but via vendor-mediated solutions rather than native capability development. Adoption will be compliance-driven, not strategic.

### Q4: Is There Hidden Architect Activity Due to Language Barrier?

**Answer**: POSSIBLE BUT IMPROBABLE

All adversarial analyses considered "hidden Architect" hypothesis. Conclusion: True Architects have international footprints (ISDA working groups, trade press). Language barrier creates 5-10% probability of hidden activity but insufficient to change classifications.

## Key Insights from Phase 3

### Insight 1: Japanese Cohort Behavior is Real

All four Japanese banks classified identically as PRAGMATIST Vendor-Dependent. This cohort behavior reflects:
- JBA coordination model
- Cultural preference for consensus
- Shared regulatory environment (JFSA, JSCC)
- Similar technology adoption patterns

**Framework Contribution**: Consider cohort-level classification for homogeneous banking sectors.

### Insight 2: Infrastructure != Strategic Adoption

JSCC CDM go-live (June 2025) created the first CCP-led CDM infrastructure requirement. However:
- Clearing members connect as consumers, not contributors
- Vendor-mediated connectivity is expected approach
- Infrastructure requirement is neutral between Architect and Pragmatist

**Framework Contribution**: Distinguish infrastructure connectivity from strategic CDM investment. CCP requirements should not inflate Architect probability.

### Insight 3: Investment Banking Insufficient for Architect

Nomura (Japan's largest investment bank) was classified PRAGMATIST despite:
- Global derivatives operations (post-Lehman)
- Higher prior than commercial peers
- Investment banking focus

Investment banking profile creates capability but does not determine strategic CDM choice.

### Insight 4: Evidence Absence is Informative

Complete absence of CDM contribution evidence across all four banks is a strong Pragmatist signal. True Architects have discoverable international footprints.
